Implemented and tested (test_rib_grillage.jl, 329 passed).

Grillage ribs are one beam per node-to-node piece, each sized on its own demand. Every rib in a sizing pass uses the thickest topping any rib bay was given. Pieces on one straight line, including across a girder, share one bar size. Each piece keeps enough bars of that size that its steel area and sagging strength do not drop. If those bars do not fit the web in one layer (ACI 318-11 §7.6.1), the piece keeps the section the sizer gave it. Stirrups stay per piece. uniform_geometry then raises every rib to the largest web and the largest depth.

A solid head's soffit is the deepest rib around it. The head's web check uses the narrowest web and the smallest effective depth. Panels around one column may differ; the joint check uses the shallower soffit rather than refusing. The scene draws the head as the filled bays, in a warmer concrete, and the pick says Solid head.

Not done: the open grid scene has not been redesigned with this. Adjacent slabs are not yet forced to the same head outline. Hogging bars are not yet placed in the topping.
