# A7 — Floor vibration serviceability

Long-span composite/steel-joist floors (offices, schools) are often **governed by walking vibration**, not strength. No vibration check exists today.

**Build:** AISC Design Guide 11 walking-excitation criterion — natural frequency, effective panel weight, peak acceleration vs. occupancy limits — as a serviceability check on floor framing produced by A1/A2. Lower priority than strength items but required for credible office/school sizing. **Depends on:** DG11 canon (Phase E, add to manifest).
