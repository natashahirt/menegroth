# Quote: negative moments at faces of supports (8.11.6.1)

The
negative moments used for design are taken at the faces of supports (rectangle section or equivalent rectangle
for circular or polygon sections) but not at distances greater than 0.175 l from the centers of supports.
ACI 318-14 (8.11.6.1)
