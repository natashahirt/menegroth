# Minimum dowel area across interface (ACI 318-14 §16.3.4.1)

Required dowel steel is the excess load divided by φfy (0.44 in.^2) but must not be less than 0.005 times the column gross area (1.62 in.^2), which governs per ACI 318-14 §16.3.4.1.
