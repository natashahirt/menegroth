# Applicability — F1(c) Cb Scope (Singly Symmetric Single Curvature, Doubly Symmetric)

The Cb lateral-torsional buckling modification factor of Eq. F1-1 applies to singly symmetric members in single curvature and to all doubly symmetric members, for nonuniform moment diagrams when both ends of the unbraced segment are braced.
