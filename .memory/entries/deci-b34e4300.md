Punching at a wall is the §11.11.1.2 section at d/2 from the reaction, with Appendix B properties. A free wall end is the three-sided edge section. Two walls that meet are the re-entrant section (slab inside the L, αs = 40) or the outside corner (αs = 20). A closed shaft is one loop (αs = 30). Vu is qu times the wall tributary area. No unbalanced moment is assigned, because the slab support fixes only the vertical displacement.

§13.7: R13.7.3.3 defines a wall as a support. §13.7.4 and §13.7.5.1 size the column and the torsional member from the column, bracket, or capital. Equivalent frame is refused when a wall supports the slab. The old §8.11 citations now point at §13.7.

Table 9.5(c) at 60 ksi: a wall on the slab boundary is a discontinuous edge, so the thickness uses ln/30 (exterior without edge beams). Footnote §'s thinner with-edge-beams column (ln/33) needs a beam with αf ≥ 0.8. A wall is not that beam.
