# Parameters Mp, hc, λ, λpw, λrw for Rpc

Mp = Fy Zx ≤ 1.6 Fy Sx; hc = twice the distance from centroid to the inside face of the compression flange (less fillet/corner radius for rolled shapes, or to the nearest fastener line/inside weld face for built-up sections); λ = hc/tw; λpw = λp (compact-web limit, Table B4.1b); λrw = λr (noncompact-web limit, Table B4.1b).
