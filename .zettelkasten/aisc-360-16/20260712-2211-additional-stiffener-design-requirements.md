# Additional stiffener design requirements (§J10.8)

AISC 360-16 §J10.8 requires transverse full-depth bearing stiffeners for compressive concentrated forces to be designed as axially compressed members (columns) per §E6.2 and §J4.4, using an effective length and the applicable cross section.
