ACI 318-11 §8.13.2 and §8.13.3 are constraints on the T-beam NLP when a center-to-center spacing is passed: rib width at least 4 in, clear spacing at most 30 in, and stem depth at most 3.5 times the width. The first two tighten the width bounds. The depth ratio is a utilization. Slab thickness stays a topping check and is not one of these constraints.

§8.13.8's 1.10 multiplies one-way Vc only while the current point meets those three limits. After snapping, the section is checked again with JoistConstruction. A snap that leaves the limits is repaired by widening first, and the increase is withheld if it still fails. Punching is not in this problem.

If no width inside the bounds can meet the limits, joist_nlp_spacing returns nothing. The building sizer then sizes that rib as a beam (§8.13.4) and does not apply the increase, instead of aborting the design. A caller that passes an impossible spacing straight to RCTBeamNLPProblem still throws.
