# Equivalent rectangular stress block and concrete compression force (§22.2.2.4.1)

The depth of the equivalent rectangular stress block is a = β1·c and the concrete compression resultant is Cc = 0.85 f′c a b, per ACI 318-14 §22.2.2.4.1.
