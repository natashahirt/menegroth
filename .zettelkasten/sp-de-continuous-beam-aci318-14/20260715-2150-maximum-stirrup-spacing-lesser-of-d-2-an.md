# Maximum stirrup spacing = lesser of d/2 and 24 in. (Table 9.7.6.2.2)

Since 4√f′c·bw·d = 89.05 kips > Vs = 43.24 kips, the maximum stirrup spacing is the lesser of d/2 and 24 in. per ACI 318-14 Table 9.7.6.2.2 → lesser of 22/2 = 11 in. and 24 in. = 11 in. (governs over demand spacing of 12.21 in.). Clause 9.7.6.2.2, page 15.
