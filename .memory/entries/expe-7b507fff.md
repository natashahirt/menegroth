Nine designs of one two-story flat-plate shear-wall office. A 20-story building in category D stops at the Table 12.2-1 160 ft limit. Category D writes the §18.7.6 note on all 12 columns; the same special row in B writes none. §18.6.5 does not appear because none of the 15 beam results has a beam section.

A longer plan (10×6 m to 14×8 m) raises concrete from 30 to 66 m³, rebar from 1793 to 3919 kg, and wall ρl from 0.0035 to 0.009. A third story and a 6 m story height also add concrete and rebar. The column section stays 196 in² at ρ = 0.0122 in every case.

Doubling the cell live load, raising SDS from 0.20 to 1.0, and dropping f′c from 4000 to 3000 psi leave the reported section, rebar, and wall ρl unchanged. Wall thickness was held at the given 250 mm. Every design reported its checks passing.
