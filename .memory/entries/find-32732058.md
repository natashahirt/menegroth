User asked why e0c0 'Beam 44' looks rectangular while mirror-symmetric 'Beam 11' is a T, and noted a deflection asymmetry. Diagnostic (sdf_e0c0_flange_diag.jl, iter12 cache ON) findings:

1. CORRECTION: Beam 44 is NOT rectangular. It is a T-beam whose effective flange collapsed to its web: section T20x30-bf20 (bw=bf=20in, zero overhang) -> renders as a bare rectangle. Beam 11 is T18x36-bf42 (24in overhang). Both were correctly detected as one-sided-flange girders (one_side=true, hf=0.1016m, any_nothing=false, identical span 7.81m and nearest parallel web 7.68m). So flange DETECTION is symmetric; my earlier 'detection artifact' hypothesis was wrong.

2. REAL asymmetry is in the DEMANDS: Beam 11 Mu=1033.5 kN*m, check_Mu=1222.0, Vu=317.1, governing=flexure_neg (hogging). Beam 44 Mu=745.3, check_Mu=605.6, Vu=237.0, governing=flexure (sagging). ~65% moment difference + opposite governing action -> different sized sections -> different depth (36 vs 30 in) -> the deflection asymmetry the user saw.

3. CHIRALITY SIGNATURE: the 4 diagonal girders from the central hub (12,10): NE(Beam11)=T18x36-bf42, SW(Beam55)=T18x36-bf42, NW(Beam27)=T20x30-bf20, SE(Beam44)=T20x30-bf20. Design is EXACTLY symmetric under 180deg rotation (NE==SW, NW==SE) but BROKEN under mirror reflection (NE!=SE across y=10, NE!=NW across x=12). Also the flange extent profiles at the SHARED node (12,10) differ: Beam11 left extent 1.92m vs Beam44 left extent 0m, and both report slab on chord-'left' when a reflection-consistent result would put them on opposite chord-sides (reflection flips handedness).

INTERPRETATION: a left/right handedness choice is baked into the per-beam derivation — the chord left-normal n=(-u_y,u_x) used by beam_side_profiles, and/or the signed moment-field sagging/hogging convention. These are rotation-covariant but NOT reflection-covariant, so 180deg-related members stay consistent while mirror-related members diverge. OPEN QUESTION: is e0c0 meant to be reflection-symmetric (=> genuine bug) or is its rib/girder pattern chiral by design (=> physical and correct)? Local star/hub patterns look non-chiral, favoring 'genuine handedness bug'. Principled fix (per user: fix the mechanism, don't force symmetry): pick the slab-bearing side from actual slab geometry and make sagging/hogging sampling orientation-independent, so the derivation is reflection-covariant by construction. Next confirm: dump beam_side_profiles + signed moment field for Beam 11 vs Beam 44 to localize the handedness to side-profile vs moment-field.
