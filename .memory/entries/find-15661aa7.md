## Failure
steel_deck on topology/r2c1 fails: group 1 'No feasible sections', L=6.0 m, Mux=384 kN·m, every section fails deflection_ll (W14X257 closest at 2.75x L/360, 'per-element composite inertia'). Different code path from the steel_rib_composite story-shell issue (find-e8f16711): this is the composite-deck GRILLAGE (frame/tributary), not the story shell.

## Ruled out: cantilever/overhang
Section-independent dump of every frame line's deflection spans (diag_overhang_geometry.jl, SS._deflection_spans): 104 chord spans, **0 tip (cantilever) spans**. So the 15.9 m² slab-edge overhang does NOT create cantilever framing, and the earlier nominal-section 'CANTILEVER' labels (diag_steel_deck_deflection.jl) were an artifact of undersized members dragging their supports down (absolute node uz ~0.6 m), not real cantilevers.

## Root cause: sparse columns -> long chord spans
topology/r2c1 supports columns only every 12 m on frame lines 1,15,16,24,25 and every 10 m on 14,19,23 (most other lines are 3 m/4.5 m/6 m). Beams are subdivided into 6 m pieces, so the error's 'L=6.0 m' member is HALF of a 12 m support-to-support chord; its live-load deflection is measured over the full 12 m chord (l=span). A 12 m floor span at L/360 is demanding and the composite girders there don't meet it as sized -> every section fails.

## Still pending (confirmation)
diag_steel_deck_strength_only.jl sizes steel_deck with the deflection limit DISABLED (completes on strength) then measures real δ/l. Was still running at ~47 min (grillage is slow) when this was recorded. It will show whether strength-sized DEEP sections on the 12 m spans still exceed L/360 (=> genuinely too-long spans; need intermediate columns / relaxed limit) or pass (=> the deflection-governed sizer wasn't reaching deep enough = a sizing issue).
