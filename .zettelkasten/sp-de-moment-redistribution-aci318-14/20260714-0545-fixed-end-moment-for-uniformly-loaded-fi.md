# Fixed-end moment for uniformly loaded fixed-end beam FEM = wl²/12

Fixed-end moment for a beam with uniformly distributed load and fixed ends. LaTeX: FEM = (w l^2)/12. Symbols: w = uniform load, l = span length. Numerically verified: for member AB, w=2.12, l=25 → FEM = 110.44 kip-ft.
