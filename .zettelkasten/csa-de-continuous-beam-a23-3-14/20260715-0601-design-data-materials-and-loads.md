# Design data (materials and loads)

Design inputs are f'c = 25 MPa normal-weight concrete (γc = 24 kN/m³), fy = 400 MPa, SDL = 1 kN/m², and typical-floor live load Lo = 2.5 kN/m² (reduced 1.6 kN/m²).
