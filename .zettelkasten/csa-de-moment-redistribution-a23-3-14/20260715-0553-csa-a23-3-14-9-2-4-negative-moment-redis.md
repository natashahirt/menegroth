# CSA A23.3-14 (9.2.4) — negative-moment redistribution limit

Except where approximate moment values are used, elastically computed negative moments at supports of continuous flexural members may each be increased or decreased by not more than (30 − 50c/d)%, but not more than 20%, with the modified negative moments then used to compute span moments.
