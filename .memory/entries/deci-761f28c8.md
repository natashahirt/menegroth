A cut bay smaller than half a module stays a ribbed void unless a column head claims it. The offcut is formed with a smaller pan, not cast solid.

This replaces the plan line that said every such bay is cast solid. `undersized_bays` only lists them. The materialize warning already says a bay no head claims stays a ribbed void. ACI 318-11 §8.13 sets no minimum bay size.
