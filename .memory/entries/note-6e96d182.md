## Opportunity

Aligns with deci-bc4e27e2 (unify column+rib capacity caching under one content-addressed catalog). Exactness-sensitive: must fingerprint the flange-width closure's underlying data, and only hit on bit-identical inputs. Expected gain modest (saves the confirming iterations after convergence), not the full 168s.
