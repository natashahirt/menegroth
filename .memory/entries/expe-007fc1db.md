grid/x1y1 dense waffle (30 in pans, 16 slabs) with rib_head_stirrups at its default true. All 16 solid heads passed on the first cast (stop=passed, steps=0, ok=true). None stopped unsized and none hit the solid cap. Governing ratios were 0.70 to 0.95, from rib hogging on twelve slabs and column punching on four. Reported one-way shear was 1.12 to 2.71 and did not govern, because the ribs carry that shear with stirrups. Punching was 0.23 to 0.76.

The building did not pass. The summary named Beam 1144, minimum reinforcement, ratio 1.0. Runtime was 2385 s. The earlier concrete-only run had most heads at the solid cap with one-way ratios 1.04 to 1.81 and five slabs stopped unsized.
