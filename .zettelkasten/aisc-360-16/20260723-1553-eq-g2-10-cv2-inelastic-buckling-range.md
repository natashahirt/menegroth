# Eq. G2-10 — Cv2 (Inelastic Buckling Range)

$C_{v2} = \dfrac{1.10\sqrt{k_v E/F_y}}{h/t_w}$

For $1.10\sqrt{k_v E/F_y} < h/t_w \le 1.37\sqrt{k_v E/F_y}$.
