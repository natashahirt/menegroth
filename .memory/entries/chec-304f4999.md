Comprehensive algorithmic change to the ribbed-floor deflection design, grounded in ACI 318-11 §9.5.2 (corpus: aci-318-11, PDF p. 132-133, verified via corpus tools).

LEVER (works): continuous midspan compression steel ρ′ = As′/(bw·d) lowers the long-term multiplier λΔ = ξ/(1+50ρ′) (Eq. 9-11, §9.5.2.5; R9.5.2.5 credits compression steel for creep/shrinkage). The T-rib ρ′ uses bw·d, matching the repo's existing design_tbeam_deflection convention. Also added the R9.5.2.6 pre-attachment deduction (−pre·ΔD on the after-attachment limit), default 0.

SEARCH: screen_rib_stems! now does a 2-D search over (stem depth × ρ′ from params.rib_comp_steel_ratios = [0,0.005,0.01,0.015,0.02]), picking the least TOTAL embodied carbon (rib stems/bars + added top steel via story_comp_steel_carbon) that meets Table 9.5(b). Ascending-ρ′ gives each stem its optimum at first pass; ascending-carbon stems with a bare-carbon ≥ best-total prune the rest. Fallback keeps deepest stem + richest ρ′.

RESULT: grid/x1y1 dense_waffle rib service deflection 1.5 → 0.88 of l/480 (:sensitive), ok=true, converged=true on every story at ρ′=0.02; no deflection-failure or convergence warnings. Overall still pass=false but ONLY on Beam 138 (is_feasible 1.51, a girder; separate, user-deprioritized). Tests: StructuralSynthesizer analyze/{rib_deflection,rib_grillage,modular_ribs} 985/985 pass, clean precompile. Reused existing _element_length_m (solid_head_check.jl) to avoid a method-overwrite precompile error. Registered the two new DesignParameters fields in scene/variants.jl.

KNOWN LIMITATION (follow-up): the screen evaluates deflection on early member-loop demands, which are more pessimistic than the authoritative report_rib_deflection! (post-loop). So the screen falls back to deepest stem + max ρ′ (0.02) rather than finding a cheaper feasible combo — the design is feasible and safe but heavier than optimal. A later screen round on report-time demands could pick a lighter (stem, ρ′).
