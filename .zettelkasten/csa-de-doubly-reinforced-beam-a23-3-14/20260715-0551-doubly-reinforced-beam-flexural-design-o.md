# Doubly reinforced beam flexural design objective

The example sizes flexural steel for a rectangular RC beam by first checking a singly reinforced section, then adding compression reinforcement (doubly reinforced) when the tension ratio ρ exceeds ~80% of ρb, with the goal of a tension-controlled section per CSA A23.3-14.
