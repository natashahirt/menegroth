# Commentary — ASTM A6 Shapes With Noncompact Flanges (F3)

A User Note lists the ASTM A6 shapes with noncompact flanges at Fy=50 ksi; all other ASTM A6 W/S/M shapes have compact flanges at Fy≤50 ksi.
