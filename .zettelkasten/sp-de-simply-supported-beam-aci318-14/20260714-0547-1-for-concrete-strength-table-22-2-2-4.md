# β1 for concrete strength (Table 22.2.2.4.3)

For f'c between 4000 and 8000 psi, β1 = 0.85 − 0.05(f'c − 4000)/1000. LaTeX: \beta_1 = 0.85 - \dfrac{0.05\,(f'_c - 4000)}{1000}. Values 0.85, 0.05, 4000, 1000 are legible verbatim.
