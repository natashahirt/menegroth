# Web sidesway buckling nominal strength (§J10.4, Eq. J10-6, J10-7)

\begin{aligned} R_n &= \frac{C_r\,t_w^3\,t_f}{h^2}\left[1 + 0.4\left(\frac{h/t_w}{L_b/b_f}\right)^3\right] & (\text{J10-6, comp. flange restrained})\\ R_n &= \frac{C_r\,t_w^3\,t_f}{h^2}\left[0.4\left(\frac{h/t_w}{L_b/b_f}\right)^3\right] & (\text{J10-7, comp. flange not restrained}) \end{aligned}
