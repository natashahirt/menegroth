# Effective moment of inertia rationale (24.2.3.7)

Immediate deflections are computed with elastic equations using the effective moment of inertia Ie (Eq. 24.2.3.5a) at midspan for simple spans; Ie provides a transition between Ig and Icr as a function of the ratio Mcr/Ma.
