# Explanatory Notes N9.2.4 — mechanism of moment redistribution

Redistribution of elastic moments occurs before failure via inelastic rotation in high-moment regions; sections with low tension-reinforcement ratios or compression reinforcement have low c/d and tolerate larger redistribution; reducing support moments (with compensating span-moment increase to keep equilibrium) can reduce required steel at both support and span.
