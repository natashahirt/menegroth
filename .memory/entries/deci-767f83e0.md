Drift (ASCE 7-22 §12.8.6.5, p. 188; commentary C12.8.6, p. 729: on flexible diaphragms the displacement at the reference location due to diaphragm deformation may be neglected).
- Reference point is the center of mass. With diaphragm deformation neglected, its displacement is the straight-line interpolation between the two wall lines on either side of it, along the axis perpendicular to the load.
- Line displacement: the largest wall displacement on that line (user choice, conservative).
- Center of mass outside the outermost wall lines in a direction: refuse with a message. No extrapolation (user choice).
- Story below is read at the vertical projection of the upper center of mass (permitted by §12.8.6.5; user choice).
- δDE = Cd δe / Ie (Eq. 12.8-16) with δdi neglected for drift per the commentary.
- Torsional irregularity ratio does not apply to flexible diaphragms (§12.3.2.1.1), so the edge-drift rule for Type 1 is not used.
- The same drift sets θ (§12.8.7: story stiffness at the §12.8.6.5 location). A flexible floor has no second-order stiffness, so for 0.10 < θ ≤ θmax forces and drifts take 1/(1−θ); θ > θmax is refused.

Force split (§12.8.4.1, p. 187: forces follow the position and distribution of the supported masses).
- Split each story force by tributary seismic weight instead of plan area. Cell weight is spread uniformly over its face. Weight lumped at elements is assigned where it sits.

Once drift is computed, FLEXIBLE_DRIFT_UNCHECKED should no longer fail a flexible design.
