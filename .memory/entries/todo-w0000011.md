# Cantilever retaining walls — soil demand + global stability

A distinct wall family: the demand is **lateral earth pressure** (not wind/
seismic story shear), and design is governed as much by **global stability**
(overturning, sliding, bearing) as by RC section strength. The corpus has one
worked example.

**Build** (new `StructuralSizer/src/walls/retaining.jl`):
- Demand: active/at-rest earth pressure (Rankine/Coulomb), surcharge, water
  table; overturning + sliding + soil bearing resultants. Pulls the industry
  soil guides already in the corpus (`industry-soil-pressure-load-factors`,
  `industry-soil-subgrade-modulus`).
- Global stability factors of safety: overturning, sliding (incl. key), bearing
  (`q ≤ qallow`, no uplift / resultant in middle-third).
- RC design of stem (out-of-plane flexure — reuse w0000008), heel, and toe
  (one-way shear + flexure, ACI 318-19 Ch. 11/13).

**Known-answer oracle** (corpus): `sp-de-cantilever-retaining-wall-aci19`.

Independent of the shear-wall/LFRS stack (different demand + limit states);
schedule after the core LFRS elements unless a foundation/retaining use case
takes priority. Overlaps the foundations work (bearing, sliding).
