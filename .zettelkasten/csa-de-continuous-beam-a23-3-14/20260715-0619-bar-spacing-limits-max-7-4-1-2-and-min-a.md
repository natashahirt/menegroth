# Bar spacing limits — max (§7.4.1.2) and min (Annex A §6.6.5.2)

Maximum reinforcement spacing is limited per CSA A23.3-14 §7.4.1.2 (smax = lesser of 3× slab thickness or 500 mm) and minimum center-to-center spacing per Annex A §6.6.5.2 (governed here to 42 mm).
