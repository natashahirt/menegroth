# G1 Default Shear Resistance/Safety Factors

Except for rolled I-shaped members qualifying under G2.1(a), design shear strength uses φv = 0.90 (LRFD) and allowable shear strength uses Ωv = 1.67 (ASD). Section G1(a) states these factors apply to all Chapter G provisions other than G2.1(a).
