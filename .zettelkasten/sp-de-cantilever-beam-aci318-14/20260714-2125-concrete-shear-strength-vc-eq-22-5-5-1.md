# Concrete Shear Strength Vc (Eq. 22.5.5.1)

Nominal concrete shear strength Vc = 2√f′c·bw·d; with φ=0.75 the design value φVc = 0.75 × 2√4000 × 16 × 21.44 = 32.54 kips. Since Vu@d = 33.6 kips > φVc/2 = 16.27 kips, shear reinforcement is required.
