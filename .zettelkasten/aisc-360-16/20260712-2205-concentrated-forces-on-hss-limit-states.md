# Concentrated forces on HSS limit states (§K2)

AISC 360-16 §K2 governs concentrated forces on HSS: plate-to-round-HSS strengths (Table K2.1) are checked for the limit states of HSS local yielding and HSS plastification, while concentrated loads on rectangular HSS defer to Chapter J.
