# Additional long-term (creep+shrinkage) deflection Δcs = λΔ·(Δsust)Inst (§24.2.4.1)

Additional time-dependent deflection from creep and shrinkage. LaTeX: \( \Delta_{cs} = \lambda_\Delta \,(\Delta_{sust})_{Inst} \). Symbols: \(\lambda_\Delta\) = time-dependent multiplier, \((\Delta_{sust})_{Inst}\) = immediate deflection due to sustained load. Worked (PCA superposition): Δcs = 2×0.762 = 1.52 in. (Source shown as PCA Notes on ACI 318-11, 9.5.2.5 Eq. 4; ACI 318-14 §24.2.4.1.)
