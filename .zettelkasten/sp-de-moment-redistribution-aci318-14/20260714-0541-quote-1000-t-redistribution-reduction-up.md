# Quote: 1000ε_t redistribution reduction up to 20% (6.6.5.3)

The ACI code allows the reduction of factored moments calculated by elastic theory at sections of maximum
negative or maximum positive moment in any span of continuous flexural members for any assumed loading
arrangement by a percentage equal to 1000 ε up to a maximum of 20 percent. ACI 318-14 (6.6.5.3)
