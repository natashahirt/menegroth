# S3 - FEM lateral (Asap shells)

The general answer for skew walls and irregular layouts.

- Wire `shell_elements` into `Asap.Model(nodes, frame_elements, shell_elements, loads)` in `to_asap!` (today they are created and then dropped).
- Mesh vertical walls as `ShellTri3` (reuse the `Shell(points, tris, section, :yz_plane/:xz_plane)` pattern) sharing nodes with floor diaphragm shells.
- Apply story lateral loads; recover per-wall in-plane `Nxy/Nxx/Mxx` via `ShellInternalForces`; integrate to wall `Vu/Mu` and feed the S1 checker.
- Optional second-order via Asap `solve_pdelta!` on the mixed model.
- **Validation:** add an Asap benchmark (single-story frame + wall shells + diaphragm under lateral load) and cross-check the wall shear split against the S2 rigid-diaphragm closed form.

Skew and torsion fall out of the FE assembly automatically - no projection math. Biggest lift (menegroth wall meshing + diaphragm wiring + wall-specific FE validation, none of which exist today).
