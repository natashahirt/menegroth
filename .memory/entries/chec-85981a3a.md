The page image of ASCE 7-22 §12.8.3 (source_id asce-7-22, PDF page 186) confirms Eq. 12.8-13. Numerator is w_x h_x^k. The denominator is a sum of w_i h_i^k with i=1 printed above the summation sign and n below it. The same order is in the glyph positions and in a crop of the sign. C12.8.3 does not restate the limits. The printed order is the reverse of the usual sum, so the clause stays held and is not encoded. k is 1 at T ≤ 0.5 s, 2 at T ≥ 2.5 s, and 2 or linear interpolation between those periods.
