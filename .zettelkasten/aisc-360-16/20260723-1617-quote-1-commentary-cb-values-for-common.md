# Quote 1: Commentary — Cb Values for Common Moment Diagrams

User Note: For doubly symmetric members with no transverse loading
between brace points, Equation F1-1 reduces to 1.0 for the case of equal end
moments of opposite sign (uniform moment), 2.27 for the case of equal end
moments of the same sign (reverse curvature bending), and to 1.67 when one
end moment equals zero. For singly symmetric members, a more detailed
analysis for C is presented in the Commentary. The Commentary provides
b
additional equations for C that provide improved characterization of the
b
effects of a variety of member boundary conditions.
