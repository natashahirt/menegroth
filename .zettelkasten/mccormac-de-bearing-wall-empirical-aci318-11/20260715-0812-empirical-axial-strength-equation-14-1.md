# Empirical wall axial-strength equation (ACI Eq. 14-1)

$$\phi P_{nw} = 0.55\,\phi\,f'_c\,A_g\left[1 - \left(\frac{k\,l_c}{32h}\right)^2\right]$$

with φ = 0.65; Ag = gross area of the wall section (in.²); lc = vertical
distance between supports (in.); h = overall thickness (in.); k = effective
length factor (Table 18.1). The number 0.55 is an eccentricity factor that
makes the equation yield a strength ≈ the axial-load-plus-bending result of
code Chapter 10 at eccentricity e = h/6. This is identical to ACI 318-19
Eq. (11.5.3.1).
