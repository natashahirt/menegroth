# Moment redistribution limit ±(30 − 50c/d)%, ≤ 20% (§9.2.4)

Elastic negative support moments may be increased/decreased by at most (30 − 50c/d)% but not more than 20%, with modified moments used for span sections, per CSA A23.3-14 §9.2.4.
