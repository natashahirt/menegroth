# Minimum clear spacing of longitudinal bars (Annex A 6.6.5.2)

Minimum clear spacing of longitudinal reinforcement is the max of 1.4·db, 1.4·a_max, and 30 mm per CSA A23.3-14 (Annex A 6.6.5.2); provided spacing (65.93 mm) exceeds smin (41.86 mm).
