# H3 — Steel seismic detailing

For moderate/high seismic (SDC C+), steel frames need **capacity design + ductile detailing**, not just strength. AISC 341-16 special/intermediate/ordinary systems (SCBF/OCBF, SMF/IMF/OMF, EBF), protected zones, expected-strength (Ry) capacity design, and AISC 358 **prequalified moment connections** (RBS, bolted flange plate, etc.).

**Build:** system-classification (R/Ω0/Cd) hooks, capacity-design force amplification, detailing checks layered on H1/H2. **Canon:** AISC 341-16 + 358 (Phase E8 ingestion — prerequisite). **Couples to:** H1, H2, D5, and hospital/high-rise typologies (F5). Deferred until H1/H2 strength design is solid.
