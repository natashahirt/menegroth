# Effective Moment of Inertia Rationale (Ig–Icr Transition)

The effective moment of inertia Ie was developed to provide a transition between the upper and lower bounds Ig and Icr as a function of the ratio Mcr/Ma, accounting for cracking effects on flexural stiffness.
