# HSS connection available-strength basis (§K1)

AISC 360-16 §K1 determines the design strength (φR_n, φM_n, φP_n) and allowable strength (R_n/Ω, M_n/Ω, P_n/Ω) of HSS connections from the governing limit-state provisions of Chapter K together with the applicable sections of Chapter J.
