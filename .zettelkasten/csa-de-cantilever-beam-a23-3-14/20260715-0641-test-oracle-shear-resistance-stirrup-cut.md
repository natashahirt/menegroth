# TEST-ORACLE — shear resistance, stirrup cutoff, and layout

With 10M @ 335 mm, Vr = Vs + Vc = 144.48 + 123.42 = 267.89 kN > Vf@dv = 145.75 kN; stirrups may stop where Vf = Vc (x = 191.52 mm), use 8–10M @ 335 mm with first stirrup 76.20 mm from the support face.
