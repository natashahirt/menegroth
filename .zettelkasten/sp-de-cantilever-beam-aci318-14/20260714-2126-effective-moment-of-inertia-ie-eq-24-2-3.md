# Effective Moment of Inertia Ie (Eq. 24.2.3.5a)

For a cracked section (Mcr < Ma), Ie = (Mcr/Ma)³·Ig + [1 − (Mcr/Ma)³]·Icr ≤ Ig; equivalently Ie = Icr + (Ig − Icr)(Mcr/Ma)³.
