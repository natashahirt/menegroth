# Phase A — Analysis engine capabilities

The **physics layer**: generating the loads that act on a building and tracing them through the structure. Today menegroth is gravity-only — it applies downward tributary loads to a frame-only Asap model and never generates wind, seismic, or snow. Almost every typology beyond a low-rise gravity frame is blocked on this.

Contains the todos that make demands *exist* and *flow* correctly:
- Finish joists-as-beams (the current active iteration).
- Composite/steel-deck floor analysis.
- ASCE 7 wind + seismic + snow demand generation.
- Diaphragm assembly & distribution (couples to the wall/lateral program `plan-w0000001`).
- Second-order (P-Δ / AISC B2) in the building model.
- Floor vibration serviceability.

This phase is the backbone; Phases B–D produce *elements*, but they cannot be checked without the demands this phase produces.
