# Deflection limits ℓ/360 (live) and ℓ/240 (long-term) (Table 24.2.2)

Serviceability deflection limits per ACI 318-14 Table 24.2.2: immediate live-load deflection ΔLL ≤ ℓ/360 (= 40×12/360 = 1.33 in.); and the portion of total deflection occurring after attachment of nonstructural elements (Δcs + ΔLL) ≤ ℓ/240 (= 40×12/240 = 2 in.). Both satisfied. Clause Table 24.2.2, pages 20–21.
