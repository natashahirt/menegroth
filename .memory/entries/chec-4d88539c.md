1000 kip / 500 kip-ft now returns :converged at 14×34 in and passes ACIColumnChecker as built. test_column_nlp.jl is 1143/1143.

Ipopt was leaving a feasible 25×25 start for a crushed, buckled section. Two stand-ins made that region easy to enter and impossible to leave.

1. ACI 318-11 §10.10.6 Eq. (10-12) (aci-318-11, p. 155), δns = Cm / (1 − Pu/(0.75 Pc)) ≥ 1, was replaced by a constant 100 once the magnifier exceeded 50. Past a magnifier of 15 the NLP follows the tangent of that hyperbola, including through the pole where the equation is undefined. When Pu ≥ 0.75 Pc that ratio stays in the strength row.
2. A collapsed φMn was reported as the axial ratio alone (about 1.4 on the section Ipopt had settled on). With moment demand the moment ratio stays in the utilization. With no moment, an axial overload is still Pu/φPn,max per §10.3.6.

The 15-magnifier switch is a numerical extension, not a code factor. The as-built checker remains the acceptance gate.
