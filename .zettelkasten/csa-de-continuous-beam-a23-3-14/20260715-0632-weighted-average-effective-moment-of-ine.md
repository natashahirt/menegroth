# Weighted-average effective moment of inertia for continuous members (Eq. 9.3 / 9.4)

$I_{e,avg} = 0.85\,I_{em} + 0.15\,I_{ec}$ (one end continuous, Eq. 9.3); $I_{e,avg} = 0.70\,I_{em} + 0.15\,(I_{e1} + I_{e2})$ (two ends continuous, Eq. 9.4)
