For each deployed poker leaf, use one removable four-member module: two upper chords from the paired leg-top nodes and two lower diagonals from the paired feet converge at one centerline apex beneath the leaf. The apex quick-connects to a locally reinforced shell contact; the complete module is removed before the leaves fold. This gives the requested cleaner converging geometry while preserving the compact dining state. The shell study must explicitly check asymmetric leaf twist and hinge demand because the former pair of outer supports is replaced by one apex.
