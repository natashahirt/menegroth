# Minimum Depth to Waive Deflection Computations (Table 9.3.1.1)

Per Table 9.3.1.1 the minimum depth for a non-prestressed cantilever beam is ℓn/8; here 100/8 = 12.5 in. Since hmin = 12.5 in < provided h = 24 in, the minimum-depth requirement is satisfied and deflection computations are not required.
