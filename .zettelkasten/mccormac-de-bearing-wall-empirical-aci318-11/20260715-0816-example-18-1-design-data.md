# Example 18.1 design data — materials, loads, geometry

Precast concrete roof beams at 7 ft 0 in. on center, bearing width 10 in. each.
Wall laterally restrained top & bottom and restrained against rotation at the
footing ⇒ `k = 0.8`. Wall clear height `lc = 16 ft 0 in. = 192 in.` Wall weight
neglected. Materials: `f'c = 3000 psi`, `fy = 60,000 psi`. Beam reaction:
`D = 30 k`, `L = 18 k`.
