Order agreed with the user on 2026-11-06. Do one at a time and test as we go.

1. Flexible-floor decisions (user decides; no code before the choice).
   - Seismic story drift on a flexible floor. ASCE 7-22 §12.8.6.5 (p. 188) reads drift at the center of mass. A flexible floor has no single representative point. Options: largest drift at any wall or column top, or a projected center of mass. Today the design fails with FLEXIBLE_DRIFT_UNCHECKED.
   - Flexible force split. §12.8.4.1 (p. 187): forces follow the position and distribution of supported mass. Today the split is by plan area, which equals the mass split only for uniform weight per area.
   - Settled already: no 5% accidental torsion on a flexible floor (§12.8.4.2.1, C12.8.4.1).

2. Walls as slab supports for core buildings. Follow plan-7f7bfe8c (core gap, one support list per slab, FEA on walls, DDM span at a wall goes to FEA, column/wall load split, punching at wall ends and core corners, Table 9.5(c) at walls).

3. Wall-building loop and speed.
   - Confirm whether a wall that is too thin is only recorded as failing. If so, add a thickness step that re-runs the lateral analysis, with a convergence check.
   - Compile time: the ordinary-wall test took 42 min on a new server, mostly LLVM on a few very large methods. Break up the largest wall-stage and lateral-analysis functions.
   - Cross-run reuse from wall_cache_contract.jl, once the optimizer calls the wall stage repeatedly.

4. Higher seismic categories. Special structural walls ACI 318-19 §18.10 (todo-701f6fca), coupling beams over core openings §18.10.7, deformation compatibility ASCE 7-22 §12.12.4 / ACI §18.14. Needs §18.10 corpus material first.

Already tracked and not in this order: wall foundations (deci-67cf61cb), detailing around openings, ties for column-like segments, a published wall-example oracle, AISC F4 reminder (todo-562ec7a4), column NLP band (todo-e9bd8f1d).
