Quick web pass (2026-10-14); every work below was confirmed to exist via search. Not yet ingested into the zettelkasten.

Mapping an unenumerable space:
- Kennedy & O'Hagan 2001, Bayesian calibration of computer models (JRSS-B); DACE tradition (Sacks et al. 1989). Emulators for deterministic simulators: probability = belief about unrun outputs.
- Gramacy & Lee 2008, Bayesian treed GPs (JASA); adaptive design follow-up (arXiv 0805.4359). Partitioned GPs for regime-switching outputs.
- Gotovos et al. 2013, Active learning for level set estimation (IJCAI). Finding where a threshold is crossed.
- Bryant & Lempert 2010 (TFSC), Lempert et al. 2008 (RAND WR-557): scenario discovery / PRIM. Interpretable input boxes predicting an outcome of interest, scored by coverage/density/interpretability. Arguably closest to human-readable institutional knowledge.
- Mouret & Clune 2015 MAP-Elites (arXiv 1504.04909); Gaier et al. SAIL (arXiv 1806.05865). Quality-diversity maps of the design space.

Exploiting known structure:
- Astudillo & Frazier, Bayesian Optimization of Function Networks (NeurIPS 2021) + grey-box BO tutorial (arXiv 2201.00272). Closest technical match: GP per node, uses intermediate outputs.
- Aglietti et al. 2020, Causal Bayesian Optimization (AISTATS).

Generalization:
- Transfer learning for BO survey (arXiv 2302.05927); Tighineanu et al. 2022 hierarchical GP transfer (AISTATS, low-data).
- Whalen & Mueller, graph-based surrogate models + transfer on trusses (arXiv 2109.02689): geometry as graph input, train across parameterizations. Relevant for geometry later.

KG-informed learning:
- Karpatne et al. 2017 Theory-guided Data Science (TKDE); KGML survey (arXiv 2403.15989): taxonomy by knowledge completeness / integration form / incorporation method.
- Hvarfner et al. 2022 piBO (ICLR): expert/KG prior steers acquisition and decays with data.
- LLM/KG causal background knowledge: Paths to Causality (KDD 2025, arXiv 2506.08771); LLM priors as soft ancestral (path-level) constraints (arXiv 2306.16902).
- Edge et al. 2024 GraphRAG (arXiv 2404.16130).

Agents:
- AgentDSE (arXiv 2606.21836): simulator-in-the-loop LLM agent, hypotheses in workspace files, up to ~2 OoM fewer sim evals; ablation: restoring variable semantics gave ~2.4x sample efficiency (as reported by the paper / summary, not re-verified).
- The AI Scientist (Nature 2026); Survey of AI Scientists (arXiv 2510.23045); EXHYTE review (Patterns 2026): bottleneck = hypothesis -> executable test -> feedback. Verification-gap audit (arXiv 2608.05179): only ~38% of runnable systems release seeds/execution traces (as reported).

Structural DSE (MIT Digital Structures):
- Tseranidis, Brown & Mueller 2016 (Automation in Construction 72): surrogates for civil structures.
- Brown & Mueller DSE toolbox (Automation in Construction); Brown & Mueller 2019 design variable analysis (IJAC 17(1)).

Impressions (not verified): no work found that combines code-derived structure + regime mapping + persistent agent memory; none handles a simulator whose code evolves; trust tiers between code provisions and learned behavior are not a concern in these fields.
