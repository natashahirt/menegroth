User decisions (2026-11-02).

1. The seismic system is selectable, and R, Omega0, Cd, and the permitted Seismic Design Categories come from ASCE 7-22 Table 12.2-1 for the declared row. R is no longer a free input. Build the shear wall-frame interactive system first.
2. The 12.2.5.8 frame requirement ("capable of resisting at least 25% of the design story shear in every story") is checked by a separate frame-only analysis at 25% of the design story shear, using the frame member checks.

Table 12.2-1 rows read from rendered PDF pages 173-175 (the table is an image; corpus text is empty there):
- A.3 bearing wall, ordinary RC shear walls: R 4, Omega0 2.5, Cd 4; B NL, C NL, D/E/F NP.
- B.6 building frame, ordinary RC shear walls: R 5, Omega0 2.5, Cd 4.5; B NL, C NL, D/E/F NP.
- C.7 ordinary RC moment frames: R 3, Omega0 3, Cd 2.5; B NL, C NP, D/E/F NP.
- E.8 dual with intermediate moment frames, ordinary RC shear walls: R 5.5, Omega0 2.5, Cd 4.5; B NL, C NL, D/E/F NP.
- F shear wall-frame interactive system with ordinary RC moment frames and ordinary RC shear walls (12.2.5.8 and 14.2): R 4.5, Omega0 2.5, Cd 4; B NL, C NP, D/E/F NP.
Footnote b: Omega0 >= 2.5 may be reduced by 0.5 for flexible diaphragms. Footnote g: shear wall = structural wall per ACI 318 2.3.

Code text:
- Definition (PDF p. 162): ordinary RC shear walls and ordinary RC moment frames designed to resist lateral forces in proportion to their rigidities, with interaction at all levels.
- 12.2.5.8 (PDF p. 179): wall shear strength at least 75% of the design story shear at each story; frames capable of at least 25% of the design story shear in every story.
- C12.2.5.8 (PDF p. 712): in SDC A or B, designing walls and frames by relative rigidity without these requirements would require a lower R than the interactive system's.
- C12.2.1 (PDF p. 708): no dual systems with ordinary moment frames; simply combining the two ordinary systems sends 12.2.3.3 to the ordinary moment frame parameters.
- 12.2.3.3 (PDF p. 177): R in a direction not greater than the least R of the systems used; Cd and Omega0 consistent with that R.

Consequences: ordinary concrete walls are NP in SDC D-F in every row, so D-F needs 18.10 special walls. The office fixture's R = 8 matches no ordinary row and must change to the declared row (ELF forces about double).
