A flat plate or flat slab whose slabs share a column now sizes in two levels. Propose designs each slab on a private copy against the preparation column sizes and does not write columns. The envelope adopts one governing column: neither side may shrink, the largest footprint wins, an equal footprint keeps the larger Ast (ACI 318-11 §10.3.6.2), and the same score keeps the earlier slab. Commit designs every slab against that frozen column and writes only the slab. The lock adopts any further growth from the commit proposals, then syncs once. A floor that is already one slab per story stays the single eachindex level.

The individual-grouping eachindex byte-identity check is retired for this path. Batch order and eachindex order still diverge; the building result is the envelope. Preparation remains a real preliminary size until the next change strips it to thickness and self-weight. Concrete columns for a wall-supported two-way floor, and ribs, come after this building run.

Typical-floor dedupe stays deferred. It is unsafe before this order exists.
