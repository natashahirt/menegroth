# Heat-straightening temperature limits (§M2.1)

AISC 360-16 §M2.1 limits local application of heat for cambering/curving/straightening so that the temperature does not exceed 1,100°F (590°C) for ASTM A514/A514M and A852/A852M steels, nor 1,200°F (650°C) for other steels.
