# Deflection limits L/360 (live) and L/240 (long-term) — Table 9.3

Per CSA A23.3-14 Table 9.3, the immediate live-load deflection limit is L/360 (=20.83 mm; ΔLL=12.84 mm o.k.) and the long-term limit is L/240 (=31.25 mm; Δcs+ΔLL=32.95 mm exceeds).
