ACI 318-19 §6.2.5.3 (PDF page 72) limits Mu including second-order effects to 1.4 times the first-order Mu. R6.2.5.3 on that page chooses 1.4 as the secondary-to-primary moment ratio: stability index Q of 0.2 corresponds to 1.25, and the ASCE 7 stability coefficient cap of 0.25 corresponds to 1.33.

The wall check was forming that ratio from the summed combination moment. On D/A1, Wall 20's dead-load moment and one earthquake sign cancel, so a 21.7 kN·m net grows to 47.9 kN·m (ratio 2.21) while each action amplifies only modestly. Story drift stays 0.21 of allowable and the pier's strength checks pass.

The comparison is now the secondary-to-primary ratio of each action that enters the moment: factored gravity (dead, live, roof companion) as one, and wind or Eh as the other. Ev stays axial and is not a moment. Strength checks keep the summed second-order forces. A floor on the net moment is not added, because a genuinely small first-order moment with a large second-order moment is the case the clause is there to catch.
