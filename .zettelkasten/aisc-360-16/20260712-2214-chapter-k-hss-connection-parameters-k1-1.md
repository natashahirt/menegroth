# Chapter K HSS connection parameters (§K1.1)

Geometric symbols for HSS connections (§K1.1): D = outside diameter of round HSS main member; D_b = outside diameter of round HSS branch; B, H = overall width and height of rectangular HSS main member; B_b, H_b = branch width and height; t, t_b = design wall thickness of main member and branch; F_c = available stress in main member; B_e, B_ep = effective widths.
