# Minimum beam depth to waive deflection (Table 9.3.1.1)

ACI 318-14 Table 9.3.1.1 gives a minimum depth for non-prestressed beams; if satisfied, explicit deflection computations may be waived. For simply supported beams hmin = ln/16.
