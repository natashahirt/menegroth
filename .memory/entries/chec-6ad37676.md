Phase 6d is in the building model. elf_story_analysis applies ASCE 7-22 §12.8 story forces to the existing rigid-diaphragm masters and solves the batch on the reduced stiffness (Asap.solve(model, cases, links), submodule 98e8298). Strength cases keep Eq. 12.8-6. Drift cases drop it and still apply Eq. 12.8-7 when S1 ≥ 0.6. Accidental torsion is Mz = ±F·e at the master, e = 5% of the story dimension perpendicular to the force.

Frame nodes were created with θz fixed, so the rigid link was tying ux and uy only and a moment at the master never entered the free system. The :rigid path now frees θz on nodes that already translate in plan. θx, θy, and supports stay fixed.

θ and the §12.8.7 multiplier come from that linear solution. θ > θmax throws. Geometric P-delta is not solved. Ax is reported from the 5% analysis and is not multiplied back into the moment, because that depends on seismic design category and Type 1 irregularity. Ie, R, SDS, S1, T, Cd, and β are caller arguments. Method :sds also needs SD1 and TL; :sa needs Sa. Wind story forces are not in this slice. Seismic weight is the dead load from effective_seismic_weight. The mass-to-sizing loop stays open for Phase 7.
