# Development-length modification factors (λ=Ψt=Ψe=1.0)

The development-length modification factors are all 1.0: λ (normal-weight concrete), Ψt (casting position, <12 in. fresh concrete below), and Ψe (uncoated bars), per ACI 318-14 Table 25.4.2.4.
