# Two-way shear stress including unbalanced moment transfer (ACI 318-14 R.8.4.4.2.3)

The factored two-way shear stress combines the direct shear stress Vu/(bo·d) with the shear stress from the transferred unbalanced moment, per ACI 318-14 R.8.4.4.2.3.
