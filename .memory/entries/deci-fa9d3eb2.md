Surface stress compression is the palette magenta (#DC267F) and tension is cerulean (#00AEEF), with the paper background at zero. The legend and the detail scale use those same hex values. Axial force and the deflection ramp are unchanged.

A cut plane is a quiet charcoal rectangle with a black plus at each corner. The plus arms follow the two axes of the plane. Where the analysis cut meets a member as a point, that mark is a plus too. The black intersection lines through the shells stay.
