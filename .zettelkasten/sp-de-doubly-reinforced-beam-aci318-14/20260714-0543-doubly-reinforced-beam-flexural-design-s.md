# Doubly reinforced beam flexural design scope (tension-controlled)

The example governs flexural design of a rectangular reinforced-concrete beam, first checking a singly reinforced tension-controlled section and, if insufficient, adding compression reinforcement to reach the required strength while staying tension-controlled.
