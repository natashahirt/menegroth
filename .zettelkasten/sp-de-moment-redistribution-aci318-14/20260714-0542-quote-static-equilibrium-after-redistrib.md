# Quote: static equilibrium after redistribution (6.6.5.4)

Static equilibrium shall be maintained after redistribution of moments for each loading arrangement. The reduced
moment shall be used for calculating redistributed moments at all other sections within the spans.
ACI 318-14 (6.6.5.4)
