# Approximate deflection methods only accurate to 20–40%

Deflection estimation methods are only accurate within a 20–40% range (Tables 9 & 10). spBeam (elastic stiffness method, beam discretized into 110 elements, accounting for adjacent spans, shape, supporting-member stiffness, and cracking) yields lower deflections than the approximate hand methods, which carry built-in conservatism.
