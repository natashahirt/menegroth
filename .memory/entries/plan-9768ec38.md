Finding behind step 1: the EFM 'M−ext = M−int = 577.9, ∑/M0 = 153%' is a labeling artifact. Each frame line reports M−ext/M−int from span 1 only, then _raise_abs takes the largest across lines under each label. Strip top steel is sized from column_moments, and wall joints are excluded from that list, so the ~580 kip·ft wall-joint hogging gets no top steel (EFM). FEA has the same gap (wall-face hogging 618–705 kip·ft is printed only). The inflated envelope does reach deflection: ρ′ is estimated from 0.75 M−int, which lowers λΔ (unconservative).

Order (user chose as listed):
1. Support moments. Envelope each line over all spans, keyed by support type (edge column, interior column, wall). Equilibrium test per span: (M−L + M−R)/2 + M+ = M0 of that span. Top steel at each wall face over the covered strip width for EFM (frame joint moment), FEA (_fea_wall_face_moments into the design result), DDM (verify column_moments includes the wall end). ρ′ from the column-face moments that get top bars (user choice). Check development of slab top bars into the wall.
2. Wall out-of-plane. Slab fixed-end moment per unit length into the wall at each floor with story axial. Out-of-plane P-M check; empirical bearing is not applicable when the resultant leaves the middle third. Ground clauses in corpus first (ACI 318-19 Ch. 11, §22.4, alternative slender-wall method). Slenderness magnifier when a gravity-only building has no rigid diaphragm.
3. Walls with openings. Replace refuse_unchecked_wall_openings! with wall_segment_layout + check_pier_segment in gravity and lateral stages. A sway story withholds the segment check with a recorded failure. Spandrels recorded as unchecked.
