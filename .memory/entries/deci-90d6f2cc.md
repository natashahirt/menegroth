The perspective camera is a 27° vertical field, a 50 mm lens. The previous 45° field is a wide lens, and on a wide window the horizontal field is wider still, so the near corner looms and the building comes to a point.

Fit places the camera from that field, so the building still fills the frame from farther away. The orthographic frame uses the same field, so switching to Top or an elevation keeps the crop. No office rerun and no commit.
