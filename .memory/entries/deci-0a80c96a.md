The sized picture of a rib floor is the designed slab: the recorded plate thickness, continuous over the rib bays, on the solved nodes. A concrete T's wide top is the effective flange, so it is folded back to the stem and the stem hangs from the soffit. The analysis line stays on the node. A floor with no slab shell keeps the flange, because it is the only plate. Solid heads, steel shapes, and rectangles are left alone.
