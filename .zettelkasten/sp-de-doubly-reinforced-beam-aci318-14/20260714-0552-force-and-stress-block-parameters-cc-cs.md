# Force and stress-block parameters (Cc, Cs, Ts, a, As, A′s, f′s)

Defines the internal-force and stress-block symbols: concrete compression Cc, added compression-steel force Cs, total tension Ts, stress-block depth a, tension steel area As, compression steel area A′s, and compression-steel stress f′s = fy at yield.
