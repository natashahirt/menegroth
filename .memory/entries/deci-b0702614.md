The drop-edge critical section is the outline of the centred drop rectangle, clipped at the slab free edges, offset by d/2. That perimeter and the area inside it are used whether or not an opening is nearby. An opening only shortens the perimeter.

The previous edge formula b0 = 2(a1 + d/2) + (2 a2 + d) left out the half-column between the centreline and the slab edge. StructurePoint's flat-slab example (sp-de-two-way-flat-slab-drop-panels-aci318-14, pages 60-62 and Table 13 on page 107) gives exterior b0 = 277.75 in = a1 + c1/2 + d/2, and the same outline for the qu*b1*b2 shear reduction. ACI 318-11 §11.11.1.2 places the section at d/2 from the actual drop edge (corpus: aci-318-11, page 200).
