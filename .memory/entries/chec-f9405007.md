§18.10.6.2 is now the boundary route at story 0 of a continuous wall line when hwcs/ℓw ≥ 2 and δu is known. Every other pier stays on §18.10.6.3, including a stocky wall, a pier above the base, a line broken by an opening, a segment beside an opening, and a skew pier.

δu is the largest absolute drift-purpose ELF design displacement along the pier axis (`:X`→`:x`, `:Y`→`:y`) among torsion `:none`, `:plus`, and `:minus` (deci-5307d29b). It is taken at the floor whose height above the base matches the top of the wall line, within 1 mm. It is not interpolated, and the P-delta multiplier is not applied. Strength-purpose cases are not read.

c is the largest §22.2 neutral-axis depth at the factored axial of the earthquake strength combinations already on the pier (`:strength_1_2D_1_0E` and `:strength_0_9D_1_0E`). R18.10.6.2 names the combination that produces δu. ASCE 7-22 §12.8.6.1 (1.0 Eh with expected gravity, not less than 1.0D+0.5L) is not a stored wall action, because drift-purpose cases are omitted from the pier forces. The stage uses the earthquake strength combinations it has and says so in the comment. §18.10.6.2(b)(i), (b)(ii), and (b)(iii) are not applied: Ve is not set equal to Vu, and the √(0.025·ℓw·c) alternative is not folded into the thickness. The 12 in. floor of §18.10.6.4(c) can open on this route, because the decision calls `compression_zone_width` with a single critical section.
