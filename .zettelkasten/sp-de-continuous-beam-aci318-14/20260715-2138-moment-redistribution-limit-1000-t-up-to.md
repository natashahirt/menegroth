# Moment redistribution limit — 1000εt up to 20% (§6.6.5.3)

ACI 318-14 §6.6.5.3 allows reduction of elastically-computed factored moments at max negative/positive sections of continuous flexural members by a percentage equal to 1000εt, up to a maximum of 20 percent. (This example does not apply redistribution.) Clause 6.6.5.3, page 9.
