# Service reinforcement stress fs = (2/3)fy (§24.3.2.1)

For crack-control spacing, the permitted service stress is taken as fs = (2/3)fy. LaTeX: \( f_s = \tfrac{2}{3} f_y \). Worked: fs = (2/3)×60,000 = 40,000 psi. Clause ACI 318-14 §24.3.2.1, page 12.
