grid/x1y1 (4x4 bays, 6x5 m) had 15 slabs, not 16. `_assign_face_holes!` matched each boundary ring by one interior point. The perimeter's interior point is its centroid (12, 10), a grid vertex, and ray casting put it in the bay x 12-18, y 10-15, which shares no vertex with the perimeter. The whole 480 m2 perimeter became that bay's hole, its net area went negative, and `initialize_cells!` skipped it as a void. No warning.

Fix: a host must also contain every vertex of the ring (`_frame_contains`, inside or on). A network's own perimeter is inside none of its bays. Regression test: 'A grid's own perimeter is not a hole of an interior bay' in test/geometry/test_slab_geometry.jl.

Pre-existing, not from this fix: 'A beam floating in a bay throws' fails because the uncommitted face-hole rewrite skips zero-area walks ('A line or a spike encloses nothing'). Needs a decision: throw, as the docstring and test say, or keep the beam with no slab load.
