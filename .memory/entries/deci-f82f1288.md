User decision, 2026-11-09: keep the FEA :frame warning when the longer span is more than twice the shorter, including a flat plate with no wall parallel to the span.

R13.6.1.2 (aci-318-11, page 252) says that panel resists the moment in the shorter span essentially as a one-way slab. The αf1 = 0 row of §13.6.4.1 does not change with ℓ2/ℓ1, so the warning is about one-way action, not a clamped percentage. Reported once: the two directions are the same ratio and its inverse. A ratio of 2 is inside §13.6.1.2 and the table, so it does not warn.

The column layout is used when it can be read (`_panel_aspect_violations`). The slab's stored spans are the maximum of each direction across cells (`governing_spans`), which can pair a long span from one cell with a long span from another and hide a one-way panel. The stored spans are the fallback, and they are the ratio the strip split uses. A parallel wall outside 0.5–2.0 still adds the §13.6.4.1 clamped-row warning.

§13.6.1.6 is not evaluated. `check_ddm_applicability` records that Eq. (13-2) applies to slabs with beams on all sides and is not implemented for flat plates (αf = 0).
