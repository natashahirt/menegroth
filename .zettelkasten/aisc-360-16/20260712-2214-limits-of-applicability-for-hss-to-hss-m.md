# Limits of applicability for HSS-to-HSS moment connections (Tables K4.1A, K4.2A)

AISC 360-16 Tables K4.1A/K4.2A bound the moment-connection strength equations by branch-to-chord width ratio (e.g. B_b/B ≥ 0.25), branch and chord wall slenderness, and aspect ratios 0.5 ≤ H_b/B_b ≤ 2.0 and 0.5 ≤ H/B ≤ 2.0.
