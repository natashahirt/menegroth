A column that lies on a wall may still be a column in another design. This office moves the sixteen core-corner column edges (four corners, four stories) from `:columns` into `:absent`. The edges and their indices stay, so moving an index back to `:columns` restores the member.

`:absent` is a reserved skeleton group. `to_asap!` keeps the frame slot and gives the edge a zero section, the same inert section a wall edge gets, so the parked line adds no stiffness and no mass. Member initialization does not build a column, beam, brace, or wall from it. The tributary split then sees only the real columns: on the 8 m bay south of the core, the two far columns keep 16 m² each and the wall keeps the 32 m² strip.

The saved v4 scene still has the stacks. Do not rerun the office design until asked.
