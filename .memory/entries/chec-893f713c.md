Increment (1) of the dual-system stage, per deci-7d2c3467's **in-loop cheap proxy** tier. No extra solve.

## What it does
`dual_frame_shear_shares(struc, set, elf, effects, mf)` (new, in dual_frame_checks.jl) measures the moment frame's share of each story shear in the already-solved COMBINED model and flags any (story,direction) below 25% (ASCE 7-22 §12.2.5.1, corpus asce-7-22 p.177).

- `set` = the first-order full-model `StorySecondOrderSet` from `_build_first_order` (u = [D,L,roof,wind…,qe…]); built ONCE in `design_dual_system!` and shared with increment-(2) `dual_frame_member_checks` (whose signature changed from `(struc,basic,wind,elf,effects,inputs,mf)` to `(struc,set,basic,elf,effects,inputs,mf)` — set now passed in, no longer built inside).
- Per seismic effect k: `uqe = set.u[3 + set.nwind + k]`; `dir = elf_direction_vector(elf, effect.direction)`.
- Per story: cut at `z = lv.elevation_m - lv.story_height_m`. `_dual_cut_shears` reuses the `_frame_cut_contributions`-style crossing logic (lower node at z, other above) but tracks elementID: sums the global horizontal shear (fx·dir1 + fy·dir2) over MF columns (ids from `_mf_column_element_ids`, via `_member_frame_elements`) as `frame_mf`, and over ALL frame columns + core pier shells (`_cut_contributions` on `struc.wall_mesh.piers`) as `total`. By equilibrium `total` = applied story shear above the cut for that case.
- `share = |frame_mf|/|total|`; keep governing (min) share per (story,direction); `passed = share ≥ 0.25`.

## Result wiring
`DualSystemResult` gains `frame_shares::Vector{FrameShareCheck}` (new struct: story, direction, frame_shear_N, total_shear_N, share, passed). `pending` drops to 1 item (just the frame-only capability gate). Diagnostics string adds the share note.

## Why share ≠ capability
Proxy is necessary but NOT sufficient (share in combined model ≠ independent 25% capability). The authoritative test remains the frame-only bare solve (increment 3, next).
