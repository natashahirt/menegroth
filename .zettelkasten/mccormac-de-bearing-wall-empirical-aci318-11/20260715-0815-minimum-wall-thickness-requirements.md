# Minimum wall thickness for empirical method (§14.5.3)

Additional requirements for empirically designed load-bearing walls:
1. Thickness ≥ 1/25 of the supported height or length (whichever is smaller),
   and not less than 4 in. (§14.5.3.1).
2. Exterior basement and foundation walls: thickness ≥ 7.5 in. (§14.5.3.2).
3. Effective horizontal length per concentrated load ≤ smaller of the
   center-to-center load spacing or (bearing width + 4h) (§14.2.4).

Matches `wall_min_thickness_si` (ACI 318-19 Table 11.3.1.1).
