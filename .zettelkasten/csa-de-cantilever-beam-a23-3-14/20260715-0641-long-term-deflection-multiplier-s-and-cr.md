# Long-term deflection multiplier s and creep-shrinkage (9.8.2.5)

Time-dependent deflection uses factor s = 2 for sustained duration ≥ 60 months and ζ = s/(1+50ρ') per CSA A23.3-14 (9.8.2.5 / Eq. 9.5), yielding Δcs = 5.32 mm and (Δtotal)lt = 10.75 mm.

CROSS-EDITION FLAG — the creep-shrinkage relation is cited in the text as "CSA A23.3-04 (N9.8.2.5)" (explanatory notes to the 2004 edition) while the multiplier s is cited to CSA A23.3-14 (9.8.2.5). Reviewer should confirm edition consistency before promoting mechanics.
