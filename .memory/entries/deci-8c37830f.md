Confirmed 2026-11-04. Clear length at a joined end is the centerline length minus (t_other/2)/sin(φ), where φ is the angle between the two wall centerlines (0 < φ ≤ 90°). At φ = 90° this is the half-thickness rule already chosen. ENGINEERING JUDGMENT: this is strip-overlap geometry, not an ACI flange-width clause.

Refuse (do not silently clamp) if the remaining clear length is non-positive — a short octagon side or a sharp parallelogram corner can vanish.
