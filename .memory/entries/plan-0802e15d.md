User, 2026-11-13. The 1.25 m waffle adapting into beams was the expected result of ACI 318-11 §8.13.4. Next, two designs of grid/x1y1, then the detailing the coarse waffle could not show.

Run, before any detailing rewrite. Leave the solid-head grower as it is so the dense waffle shows whether a legal module keeps the heads small.

1. Slab and beams, no solid heads. The existing two-way concrete path: T-beams, no grillage, so grow_building_solid_heads! does not run. The scene is that analysis model, slab included.
2. Waffle with the rib module at 30 in center-to-center, both ways. ACI 318-11 §8.13.3 (page 120, aci-318-11) limits clear spacing to 30 in. Centers of 30 in put the clear space at 30 in minus the rib width, inside that limit. The current parameter is center spacing (1.25 m is 49.2 in centers).

Then detailing, on the dense waffle:

- The topping is in the scene and in the bar drawings. Removable forms: thickness at least one-twelfth the clear rib spacing and at least 2 in (§8.13.6.1), with flexural steel across the ribs and not less than §7.12 (§8.13.6.2).
- A solid head is the pans left filled, detailed as a slab of the full rib depth, not as a beam rectangle with only top bars.
- A rib that is not inside the head stays on detail_beam, the same path as a rectangular concrete beam. The head does not replace that rib.
