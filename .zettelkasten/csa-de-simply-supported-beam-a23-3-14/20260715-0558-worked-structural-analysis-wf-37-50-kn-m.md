# Worked structural analysis — wf=37.50 kN/m, Vf=140.63 kN, Mf=263.67 kN·m

With wf=37.50 kN/m and L=7.50 m, the simply supported reactions/shear Vf = wL/2 = 140.63 kN and midspan moment Mf = wL²/8 = 263.67 kN·m.
