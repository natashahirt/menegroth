# Maximum tension steel of a tension-controlled singly reinforced section

For the tension-controlled singly reinforced limit, the tension force equals Cc so the maximum steel area is As = Cc/fy, and the resulting nominal moment Mn = Cc(d − a/2) must be compared against the required Mn.
