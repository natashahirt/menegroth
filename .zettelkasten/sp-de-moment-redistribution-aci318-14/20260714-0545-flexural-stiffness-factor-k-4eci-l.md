# Flexural stiffness factor K = 4EcI/l

Stiffness factor at a beam or column end (moment to rotate the end 1 rad). LaTeX: K = (4 E_c I)/l. Symbols: E_c = concrete modulus, I = moment of inertia, l = member length. Numerically verified for member AB: 4·3834.25·4096/(25·12) → 209.40×10^6 in.-lb.
