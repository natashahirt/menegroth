After the ordinary-wall stage, an RC frame column that fails P–M on the solved model is floored at its current catalog section and the member stage runs again, even when the wall thickness and the row-F floors did not move. The member stage sizes the frame before the walls are in the model, so a wall that stays at its starting thickness still changes the column forces. Flat-plate columns stay on the slab check. A floor the catalog does not contain is not stored. Sections that do not change end the round. Pierced segments stay rectangles: the flange belongs to the gross pier, so a flanged segment with hwcs/ℓw > 1.5 still refuses Mpr. A pier with a flange at both ends still has no section.
