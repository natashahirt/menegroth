# Averaged Ie interior span (spBeam Eq. 2-103)

Weighted average effective inertia (two continuous ends). LaTeX: \( I_{e,avg} = 0.70\,I_e^{+} + 0.15\,(I_{e1}^{-} + I_{e2}^{-}) \). Symbols: \(I_e^{+}\) = midspan Ie, \(I_{e1}^{-}, I_{e2}^{-}\) = Ie at the two negative-moment (support) regions. (Source: spBeam Manual v5.00 Eq. 2-103. Note: the source text labels this "for end span"; preserve verbatim and flag — Eq. 2-103 conventionally applies to spans continuous at both ends.)
