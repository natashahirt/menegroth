# User-note guidance: Chapter K strengths are strength-limit-state only

A User Note in Chapter K clarifies that the connection strengths of Chapter K (with the applicable sections of Chapter J) are based on strength limit states only; where excessive connection deformations may impair serviceability, the Commentary should be consulted.
