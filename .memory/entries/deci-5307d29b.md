δu for ACI 318-19 §18.10.6.2 is ElfCase.design_m at the center of mass of the floor at the top of the wall line.

That value is the ASCE 7-22 §12.8.6.3 Design Earthquake Displacement, Eq. 12.8-16: δDE = Cd·δe/Ie + δdi, with δdi = 0. It is the displacement from the base, not the story drift in design_drift_m.

The cases that supply it are purpose :drift (the drift Cs, which drops Eq. 12.8-6) and the direction along the pier's axis. When the accidental-torsion cases are present, δu is the largest design_m among torsion :none, :plus, and :minus at that level. Strength-purpose cases are not used.
