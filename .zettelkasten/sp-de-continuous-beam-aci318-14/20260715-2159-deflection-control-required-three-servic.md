# Deflection control required; three service load levels analyzed (§24.2.3)

Because the preliminary depth did not meet the minimum, deflections are computed. Immediate deflections use elastic analysis at three service load levels: D, D+Lsustained, and D+Lfull, using the effective moment of inertia Ie. Clause 24.2.3, page 16.
