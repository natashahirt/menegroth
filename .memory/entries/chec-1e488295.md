## What

Phase 2b of #7: a **caller-owned** reduced-factorization cache for the non-mutating `solve(model, cases, links)` variants that phase 2a cannot serve — the lateral stage's P-Δ (`geometric_stiffness`) and frame-only (`exclude_shells`) solves. They build reduced matrices with no counterpart in `model._factorization` and the lateral stage *interleaves* them across many load cases and gravity combinations.

## Asap (`external/Asap`)

- New `RigidLinkFactorCache` (mutable, `Dict{UInt, CHOLMOD.Factor}`) + `_cached_reduced_factor!` in `factorization_cache.jl`; exported from `Asap.jl`.
- `solve(model, cases, links; …, factor_cache=nothing)` consults the cache between the phase-2a model-factor reuse and the fresh path.
- **Cache key** = `hash((_reduced_factor_pattern_key(model, Krr, retained_free), exclude_shells, geometric_stiffness !== nothing))` — reduced pattern key **plus a variant discriminator**, so `S` / `S+K_G` / shells-excluded never share a factor even if their patterns coincide.
- Hit → `cholesky!(old, Krr)` (numeric-only, reuses symbolic ordering); miss → fresh `cholesky`. Numerics always recomputed ⇒ reuse is bit-identical to a cold factor (same exactness argument as phase 1).
- **Only SPD factors cached**; indefinite `Krr` (buckling P-Δ) falls back LDLᵀ→LU and stores nothing. Invisible to `_N_FACTORIZATIONS` (matches the rest of non-mutating `solve`); `:factor`/`:refactor` log events are the reuse signal.

## StructuralSynthesizer

- `_lateral_factor_cache(struc)` (`analyze/lateral/story_pdelta.jl`) holds the cache in `struc._analysis_caches` under reserved key `_LATERAL_FACTOR_CACHE_KEY = -3`; `clear_geometry_caches!` drops it on remesh (key also folds in `objectid(S)` — defence in depth).
- Threaded into the three per-gravity-key P-Δ loops: `ordinary_wall_stage.jl` (`K_G`, shells kept) and `interactive_checks.jl` (`K_G`+`exclude_shells`, both frame-only stability and frame-member-strength). Each sweep now pays one symbolic analysis per pattern, not one per gravity key.

## Safety

Core FEM solve under rigid diaphragms — safety-critical. The cache is exact-or-it-does-not-fire: every reuse recomputes numerics from the current `Krr`, variant+pattern keyed; any mismatch or non-SPD result falls to a fresh `cholesky`→LDLᵀ→LU, i.e. prior behaviour.
