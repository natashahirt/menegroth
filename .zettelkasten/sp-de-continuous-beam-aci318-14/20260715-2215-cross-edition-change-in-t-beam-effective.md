# Cross-edition change in T-beam effective flange width (318-11 vs 318-14)

CROSS-EDITION: In ACI 318-11 and earlier, the slab width effective as a T-beam flange was limited to one-fourth the beam span (center-to-center). In the 2014 revisions the effective width became one-eighth of the clear span on each side of the web (Table 6.3.2.1); the commentary states negligible design impact. StructurePoint software retained the earlier 318-11 basis ("more exact"). Resulting beff: 318-14 Hand = 130.5 in.; 318-11 spBeam = 120.0 in. Preserve the edition distinction.
