# F4 — Light wood-frame generator

Generator for wood-framed typologies (Small Hotel, Full/Quick-Service Restaurant, Mid-rise Apartment, single-family): repetitive **stud bearing walls + wood joist/truss floors + wood roof** (D1/C3), **wood shear walls + diaphragms** (SDPWS) for lateral, including the **5-over-1 podium** (light frame over a concrete/steel podium) common to mid-rise apartments.

**Deliverable:** sizable wood-frame + podium models + report. **Depends on:** D1 (the whole timber stack), C3, B6 (if mass-timber variant), plus A3/A4 for podium transfer. Largest new-material integration.
