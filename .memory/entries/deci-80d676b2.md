A placed bar has to carry a centerline in the member's own frame, at a resolution that could be meshed. A count plus one elevation path is not that. Ties and stirrups need the closed loop at each stored station. Longitudinal bars need each bar's place in the section, not one path shared by the count. Parallel slab, footing, and beam bars need their position across the member. The viewer does not invent those coordinates.

Columns are already detailed with closed ties (or a spiral) and crossties. The section sheet draws one tie from the section object. The elevation draws a stroke at each station. The stored tie path is that stroke, and the crossties have stations and no path.

No office rerun and no commit.
