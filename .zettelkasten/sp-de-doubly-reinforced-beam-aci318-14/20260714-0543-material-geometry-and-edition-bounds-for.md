# Material, geometry, and edition bounds for the design example

The design uses ACI 318-14 with f’c = 5,000 psi, fy = 60,000 psi, a 14 in. × 29 in. rectangular section, 3 in. cover to reinforcement centroid, under service moments M_DL = 234 kip-ft and M_LL = 414 kip-ft.
