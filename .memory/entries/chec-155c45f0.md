Wall self-weight is dead load on the wall triangles. `wall_self_weight_loads` builds one `AreaLoad` per density and thickness, id `:wall_self_weight`, pressure ρ t g with g = 9.81 m/s² (the Asap.SelfWeight value, not a code factor). Each triangle splits pressure times its area equally to its three nodes, downward. Shells of different ρ or t are not pooled.

`to_asap!` multiplies that pressure by the dead factor the slab dead load in the same solve already uses: `combo.D` when dead and live are split, otherwise the dead-only envelope (the largest D factor). `sync_asap!` rewrites the pressure from the shells. Pattern cases keep the load with dead. Slab area-load refresh skips `:wall_self_weight`.

A horizontal cut of the triangles above the line equals the self-weight assigned to nodes strictly above the line. The share assigned to nodes on the line is not in that resultant. Full pier weight is the cut plus the share on the cut nodes. `analyze/test_wall_cuts.jl` 39/39.
