# Fraction of unbalanced moment transferred by shear (ACI 318-14 §8.4.2.3.2, Eq. 8.4.4.2.2)

The moment-transfer factor γf is computed per ACI 318-14 §8.4.2.3.2, and the shear-transfer fraction γv is taken as 1 − γf per Eq. 8.4.4.2.2.
