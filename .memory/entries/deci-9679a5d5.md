A solid head grows by one bay only when check_solid_head_punching failed, named a direction, and carried a positive shear. grow_checked_column_head reads that direction and that shear. It does not choose a side or a load.

One-way shear names the direction: the outward plan direction of the rib that leaves the head. Two governing ribs that leave in different directions name none. Column punching and the rib-web section report a stress and do not name a face, so those failures leave the seeded head. The frame joint stores the check's direction. The seeded bay set is not rewritten during design; the rectangle frame still sizes its head by the corner fraction.

On a 1 m module grid, a 400 kip rib to the east adds the east bay and an 800 kip rib to the north adds the north bay. The larger shear does not add a second bay in the same step. A second step in the same direction adds one more bay. A passing check, a rib with no direction, and a zero shear do not grow.
