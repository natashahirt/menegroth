# Design data — material, section, load, and bar inputs

The worked example uses fc′=30 MPa NWC, fy=400 MPa, DL=12.00 kN/m, LL=15.00 kN/m, span L=7.50 m, 30M longitudinal bars (As=700 mm², db=29.90 mm), 10M stirrups (As=100 mm², db=11.30 mm), 30 mm clear cover, 20 mm max aggregate.
