# HSS-to-HSS moment connections (§K4)

AISC 360-16 §K4 governs HSS-to-HSS moment connections (one or two branch members welded to a continuous chord loaded in bending), classified as T-, Y-, or cross-connections and checked for chord plastification, punching shear, and chord distortional failure.
