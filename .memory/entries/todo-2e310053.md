# D2 — Cold-formed steel (CFS)

CFS is **absent entirely** (no material, no sections, no checks). It is the light-gauge framing for mid-rise residential, hotels, and load-bearing partitions, and the girts/purlins of metal buildings (C5).

**Build:** AISI S100-16 effective-width / Direct Strength Method for local/distortional/global buckling; C-stud, track, and joist section catalogs; axial+bending interaction; screw/weld connections; CFS shear walls (AISI S400 / sheathed wall tables) for lateral. **Depends on:** Phase E3 (AISI S100 + S240/S400). Complex due to thin-walled buckling — scope DSM carefully.
