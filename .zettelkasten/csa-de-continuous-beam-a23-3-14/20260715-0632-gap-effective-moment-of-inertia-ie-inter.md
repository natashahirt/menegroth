# GAP — effective moment of inertia Ie interpolation (Eq. 9.1)

For cracked sections (Ma > Mcr) Ie is computed by the Branson-type interpolation of Eq. 9.1 (Ie = Icr + (Ig − Icr)(Mcr/Ma)³ ≤ Ig; uncracked Ie = Ig when Mcr > Ma), but the printed Eq. 9.1 is OCR-garbled.
