# Quote: flange tension bar distribution ln/10 (24.3.2.1)

If flanges of T-beams are in tension, part of the bonded flexural tension reinforcement shall be distributed over
an effective flange width, but not wider than l /10. Where l is the length of clear span measured face-to-face
n n
of supports. ACI 318-14 (24.3.2.1)
