Hardened the re-entrant punching convention and validated the long-term invariants (no FEA script, per user).

## Changes
1. **checks.jl**: renamed the wiring locals `Mux_g`/`Muy_g` → physically-named `M_about_x`/`M_about_y` with an explicit ⚠ convention block — kills the silent-swap risk (the mapping is now self-verifiable against the specs).
2. **punching.jl**: added a loud `!!! warning` to `punching_stress_general` documenting that its moment convention is OPPOSITE to `punching_check` (this engine: Mux about x ⇒ stress in y, per 421.1R; `punching_check`: Mux ⇒ stress in x).
3. **Tests** (test_general_critical_section.jl, now 56/56):
   - **Convention lock** against the closed form via a 'nearer-oracle' test on a 4:1 rectangle (32×8). Each mapping must land nearer its matching closed-form orientation than the other; a swap flips both and fails.
   - **Geometry invariant**: notching a corner can only raise stress (g_re.vu ≥ g_int.vu at equal b0), yet stays far below the 2-sided idealization.
   - **± transfer-sign bracket**: subtract/zero/add are monotone and the full swing is <10% of the stress.

## Empirical findings (calibration, moment-only across aspect ratios)
- The App. B `J≈I` conservative gap is only **~2-3%** (gy/vx ∈ 1.01-1.03), NOT the ~10% I had feared.
- The Mux↔Muy swap changes `vu` by at most **~8%** (peak separation at 4:1; γv calibration makes the two orientations nearly equal for near-square sections — at 1:1 they're identical). So the convention, while real at the code level, is a <~8% effect on stress and is swamped for symmetric sections.
- The direct `Vu/Ac` term is identical across all four quantities, so it cancels in every difference — the convention lock is load-independent.
- **Transfer-sign residual**: the ± swing on a representative re-entrant case is <10% of the stress, confirming the `|M|−Vu·e` direction (inherited from orthogonal-corner validation) is second-order for re-entrant sections (small centroid offset), well inside the model conservatism.

## Net
The caveat from deci-6bee9104 is quantitatively discharged for re-entrant corners without an FEA rig. Roadmap item remains: unify on the general engine, demoting the closed forms to fast-path + oracle, to retire the opposite-convention trap entirely.
