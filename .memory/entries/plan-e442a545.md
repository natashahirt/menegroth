Turn each sagging region's design_shear_anchors layout into stations and flange offsets, store them with the composite stiffness, and serialize them on the frame element.

Stations follow AISC 360-22 §I8.2d(a): n rows on a shear span sit at the centers of n equal spaces. On perpendicular or skewed deck they snap to rib crossings. The deck sheet's position along the beam is not in the model, so crossings are taken at half a pitch from station 0, then every pitch. Across the flange, n_per_row studs are spaced between the §I8.2d(b)/(d) center limits. On a skewed rib a transverse offset y moves the station by y·cot θ.

APIVisualizationFrameElement gains stud_positions [[s, y], ...] and stud_diameter, s measured from the element's start node. Stud mass and its carbon are added to the beam steel takeoff. Hogging studs stay out: production still does not design negative-moment anchors.
