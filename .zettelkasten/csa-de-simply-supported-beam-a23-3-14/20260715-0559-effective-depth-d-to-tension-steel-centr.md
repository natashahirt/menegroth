# Effective depth d to tension-steel centroid

\( d = h - \left(\text{cover} + d_{stirrup} + \tfrac{d_b}{2}\right) \); symbols: \(h\) overall depth, \(d_b\) longitudinal bar diameter, \(d_{stirrup}\) stirrup diameter (all mm).
