# Quote: spBeam widely used for beam and slab design per ACI/CSA

spBeam is widely used for analysis, design and investigation of beams, and one-way slab systems (including
standard and wide module joist systems) per latest American (ACI 318-14) and Canadian (CSA A23.3-14) codes.
