Roadmap locked 2026-10-28.

1. Finish steel composite-deck sizing first. The remaining mechanics step is beam deflection with station-varying composite stiffness along the span, instead of one stiffness for the whole member. The continuous-section catalog snap stays off this path: production beam sizing is already the discrete catalog, and plate girders stay out.

Already in place for the deck: skewed ribs, unshored gage plus published bearing, one-way shear, catalog finished-slab strength, hogging reinforcement of the concrete on the deck, and diaphragm classification.

2. After that, plan — before building — how to size a reinforced-concrete slab-and-beam rib system. The cases to cover are parallel ribs (one-way joists), an arbitrary rib orientation or network, and waffle (two-way joists). This is a concrete rib floor, not the steel deck. Existing waffle and one-way-joist todos wait on that plan.
