# Concrete cover for cast-in-place non-prestressed beams (Table 20.6.1.3.1)

1.5 in. clear concrete cover is used per ACI 318-14 Table 20.6.1.3.1. With #8 bars, effective depth d = 24 − (1.5 + 0.5×8/8) = 22 in. Clause 20.6.1.3.1, page 11.
