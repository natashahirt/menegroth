The flange of a joist or waffle rib is the slab (ACI 318-11 §8.13.1, page 120). The stem is drawn with its top at the soffit.

When the story shell already offsets the stem, the picture uses that axis and draws the stem only. When the rib is still on the joint and a slab shell is present, the picture drops the stem by half the slab thickness plus half the stem, and does not draw the flange a second time. A T-beam with no separate slab keeps its flange, because that flange is the only slab in the picture.

A rectangular beam and a steel section still seat on the top fiber. An I-beam is not treated as a rib.
