Step 1 of plan-9768ec38, slab support moments.

The 153% of M0 was one span's two ends, both labeled from span 1 and then raised across frame lines. Each span still sets its positive moment from M0 − (Mleft + Mright)/2. A span whose positive moment is more than 1 kip·ft below zero stops the analysis.

A wall joint is any support carrying `wall_fixed`, including a half-strip wall whose joint is a column. Its negative moment is M_wall_ext at an end of the frame and M_wall_int between columns. It is not filed as M_neg_ext or M_neg_int. Top steel takes that moment with the column-strip fractions. The shell's wall-face integral is added the same way on the direct strip path. ρ′ for long-term deflection uses the largest column-face or wall-face moment that receives top bars.

Open floor at z = 3.6 m, h = 10.5 in, M0 = 594.6 kip·ft:
- EFM: column exterior 264.1, column interior 0.0, positive 330.3, wall 577.9.
- Shell: column exterior 187.7, column interior 0.0, positive 321.4, wall 704.5 (the larger of 704.5 and 617.6 at the two core walls).

The wall moment is still the centerline value. Face-of-support reduction needs the shear at that wall, which the frame result does not store. Straight-bar development into the wall is not checked yet. Out-of-plane wall design, openings, and the flexible diaphragm are still ahead.
