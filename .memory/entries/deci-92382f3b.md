Confirmed by the user 2026-11-05. A low-rise building whose columns can sit on spread footings may also carry a mat under the core. That mat is one strategy, not the strategy, and not what coverage-based auto selection should pick.

The October 23 seam still stands for a tower: the core lands on the building mat, and wall base reactions go to that mat. This decision adds the other case. Column supports outside the core stay on spread footings. A support vertex inside the core footprint moves onto the mat so the two foundations do not both claim its reaction.

Analysis stays fixed-base. The mat is the rigid idealization. The wall mesh still adds no supports of its own; the foundation owns the base restraint.

Not in this strategy: replacing spread footings, a flexible mat, piles, or overturning/sliding checks.
