# 80%-of-ρb trigger for compression reinforcement (reference rule)

The reference recommends adding compression reinforcement when ρ reaches 80–85% of ρb; 80% is used here, and since ρ(0.0232) > 0.80ρb(0.0210), compression reinforcement is recommended.
