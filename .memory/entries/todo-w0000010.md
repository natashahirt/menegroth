# Special structural walls — boundary elements & seismic detailing

The current checker is an **ordinary** wall (ACI 318-19 Ch. 11). Walls in
seismic force-resisting systems (SDC D–F) are **special structural walls**
(§18.10) with extra strength, detailing, and boundary-element requirements —
the part that actually governs high-seismic design and drives confinement steel.

**Build** (new `StructuralSizer/src/walls/special.jl`):
- Special-wall shear: §18.10.4 with the per-pier / shared-pier caps
  `Vn ≤ 10·Acv·√f'c` (individual) and `8·Acv·√f'c` (all piers) — note the S1 cap
  we just fixed is the Ch. 11 = §18.10.4.4 individual form.
- Displacement-based **boundary element trigger** §18.10.6.2 (`c ≥ lw/(600·(δu/hw))`)
  and the stress-based alternative §18.10.6.3 (`> 0.2f'c`).
- Boundary-element extent, transverse confinement (§18.10.6.4), and vertical
  spacing / detailing checks.
- `CheckResult` clauses `ACI 318-19 18.10.x`.

**Canon**: §18.10 is NOT yet in the extracted corpus (`corpus/text/`) — the ACI
examples are ordinary/tilt-up/bearing. **Gather canon first** (add ACI 318-19
Ch. 18.10 pages or a StructurePoint special-wall example to the corpus manifest)
before implementing — stage-1 HARD STOP per the `implement-design-system` skill.
