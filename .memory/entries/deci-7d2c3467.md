## Provision (verbatim)
ASCE 7-22 §12.2.5.1 Dual System (corpus: asce-7-22, PDF page 177): “For a dual system, the moment frames shall be capable of resisting at least 25% of the design seismic forces. The total seismic force resistance is to be provided by the combination of the moment frames and the shear walls or braced frames in proportion to their rigidities.” Commentary C12.2.5.1 (PDF page 710): the 25% frame is a judgment-based secondary SFRS for redundancy/ductility.

Two distinct demands: (a) the COMBINED model distributes force by rigidity (what the main analysis already does), and (b) the moment frame must be independently CAPABLE of 25%.

## Decision (user, cost-aware hybrid)
Enforce (b) in two tiers, staged by expense:
- **In-loop cheap proxy**: during sizing iterations, require the moment frame's share of each story shear in the COMBINED analysis to be ≥ 25%. No extra solve; mirrors the existing row-F 75/25 pattern. Fast fail / steering signal.
- **Capability gate (authoritative)**: as the final DualSystem `system_checks`, clone the lateral model with the core/walls removed, apply 25% of the design base shear (per direction), and verify the bare moment frame is feasible (strength + the already-wired AISC/ACI second-order column demands). This is the literal “capable of resisting” test; one extra linear analysis per direction, run once at the gate rather than every iteration.

The combined-share proxy is necessary but NOT sufficient (share ≠ independent capability); the separate frame-only analysis is the governing check.

## Implementation placement
- `seismic_members(struc, ::DualSystem)` = (walls=core piers, beams=frame beams, columns=frame columns).
- `prepare_lateral_model!(struc, ::DualSystem)` refuses a building missing either the moment frame or the walls/core; core reuses the existing wall FEM (user's earlier reuse_wall_model choice).
- `system_checks(struc, ::DualSystem; sdc)` runs the capability gate.
- Cheap share exposed as a standalone (e.g. `frame_shear_share`) for the sizing loop.

## Drift-governor wiring notes (carried)
- Seismic drift constraint must use the DESIGN story drift Cd·δxe/Ie (§12.8.6): pass δ_allow = Δa·Ie/Cd to the elastic governor; wind stays elastic.
- Core is the governor ‘background’ (non-sized); δ_eff = δ_allow − core drift. If ≤ 0 → stiffen core, not frame.
- w_e from steel/RC catalog frontier dWeight/dI (not the length proxy used in tests).
