Do not bake a fixed core/perimeter split (the 50% rule of thumb) into wall sizing. Walls are sized for the forces the analysis assigns them.

Today the fast lateral model (`ReducedDiaphragmEvaluator`) sums story stiffness from walls only, so an applied story shear is shared among walls and the perimeter frame is invisible. The building model (`to_asap!`) already contains frame members and wall shells on one diaphragm, so a solved share there is stiffness, not an assigned percentage. A shear-wall building whose perimeter is gravity framing is designed with the walls taking the analyzed story shear. A dual system is a later choice: ASCE 7-22 §12.2.5.1 requires the moment frame to be capable of at least 25% of the design seismic forces on its own, and the commentary says that percentage is judgment (corpus: asce-7-22, PDF page 710). The walls and frame together still resist the full seismic force from one combined analysis. The 25% check is not the elastic share and does not apply to wind.

Revisit this when perimeter lateral systems are added (moment frames, bracing, or anything else that is part of the lateral system). Build an iterative sizing loop, ideally a fast one, so frame, brace, and wall sizes and the redistributed forces converge together. The fast story stiffness has to include those systems, not only walls. The combined frame-shell model stays the check on the result. Phase 7 ordinary-wall sizing does not wait on that loop.
