# Parameters Aw, h, t, Cv2 for Rectangular HSS/Box (G4)

Aw = 2ht; Cv2 per G2.2 with h/tw=h/t and kv=5; h = clear distance between flanges less inside corner radius (HSS) or between flanges (box), else outside dim − 3t; t = design wall thickness per B4.2.
