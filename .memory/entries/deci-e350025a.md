`DesignParameters.flat_plate_analysis` defaults to `:building`. A flat plate or flat slab is then analysed as loaded shells of the building model (`FEA(source = :building)`) instead of a separate slab model. `:options` keeps the method the floor options name. The building package fills the FEA cache from one factorization: unfactored dead, unfactored live, and per-cell live when pattern loading is on. `building_fea_available` falls back to the slab's own model when the building has no shells for it.

Also in this commit: `rib_head_stirrups` defaults to true, so a rib into a solid head carries shear with stirrups (ACI 318-11 §11.1.1) and the head grows for punching and hogging. The Asap pointer moves to b066f93. Scene variants register `:flat_plate_analysis` on the slab.

Left unstaged: `scripts/runners/diag_head_unsized.jl` (rib pieces cut by a solid head staying unsized), `StructuralStudies/test/test_framing_hierarchy.jl`, and a generated viewer scene. The Asap submodule still has an uncommitted `head.exe.stackdump`.
