ACI 318-19 PDF pages 326–332 (§18.10.6.2 through the start of §18.10.7) were garbled because the Times italic and bold faces have no ToUnicode map. The roman face on page 326 has a T42UV map (space at cid 3, digits at 19, A at 36, a at 68). Ligatures and symbols were read off the drawn glyphs: fi/fl/ff/ffi, slash, ≥, δ, prime, ℓ, λ, ρ. That fallback is `_aci_cid_fallback` in scripts/util/convert_pdfs_to_text.py. `scripts/runners/reextract_aci_318_19_pages.py` replaced those PAGE blocks in corpus/text/codes/aci/ACI-318-19.txt. Fraction joining was left off; a hairline was binding into the prose ("derived" was being rewritten). The replaced span has no remaining (cid:) tokens.

Now readable, corpus aci-318-19 pages 326–328: §18.10.6.2 applies to walls or wall piers with hwcs/ℓw ≥ 2.0 that are effectively continuous from the base of structure to top of wall, with one critical section for flexure and axial load. (b)(i) extends the transverse reinforcement above and below the critical section by the greater of ℓw and Mu/4Vu. (iii) requires δc/hwcs ≥ 1.5 δu/hwcs, with δc/hwcs from Eq. (18.10.6.2b) and not taken below 0.015. §18.10.6.3 triggers a special boundary element where the extreme-fiber compressive stress exceeds 0.2f′c and allows it to stop below 0.15f′c. §18.10.6.4(a) extends the element horizontally by the greater of c − 0.1ℓw and c/2. The text layer of (b)(i) reads "a least", not "at least". Equations stay stacked across lines. The running corpus MCP process still served the old garbled page after the file was replaced.
