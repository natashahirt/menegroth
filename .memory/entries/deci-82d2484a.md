Embodied carbon only counted a member when `volumes` was non-empty. Flat-plate column sizing wrote the RC section and left `volumes` empty, except when harmonization enlarged a column, and that write stored gross area as concrete. Slab rebar was already a separate volume key; RC beams and RC columns from member sizing stored `section_area · L` under the concrete key and dropped longitudinal steel.

`member_material_volumes` now splits every sized member: structural steel is its own key; reinforced concrete is `(Ag − As) · L` of concrete plus `As · L` of rebar (`As_total` on columns, `As + As_prime` on beams). Foundations already stored concrete and rebar separately. Flat-plate slabs already stored concrete and rebar. Each `ElementECResult` carries `concrete_ec`, `rebar_ec`, and `structural_steel_ec`, and `DesignSummary` sums the same three (plus timber, other, and fireproofing) across the building. The sweep CSV reports the three material carbons.
