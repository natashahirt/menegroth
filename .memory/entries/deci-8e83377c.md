The design summary no longer uses a stand-in steel density of 7850 kg/m3 or invents 1.5% rebar for an unsplit reinforced-concrete volume. Concrete, rebar, and structural-steel quantities come from the same element volumes as the takeoff, and mass is volume times that material's density. Drop-panel, shear-cap, and capital concrete, and shear-stud steel, are written onto slab.volumes when the slab volume is updated, using the slab concrete and the floor options' stud material, so embodied carbon and the element takeoff include them once. An unsized beam is left without a section; the 12 by 18 in, four-#8 visualization section is no longer installed when beam sizing is skipped. A column that grows after the slab design still keeps its bars when ACI 318-11 section 10.9.1 allows it, and otherwise gets the smallest standard layout in that window. That path is not a moment redesign, because the slab moment is not stored on the column.
