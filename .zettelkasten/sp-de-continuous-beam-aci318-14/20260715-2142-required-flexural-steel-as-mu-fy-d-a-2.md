# Required flexural steel As = Mu / [φ·fy·(d − a/2)]

Required tension steel from moment equilibrium of the equivalent rectangular stress block. LaTeX: \( A_s = \dfrac{M_u}{\phi\,f_y\,(d - a/2)} \). Symbols: \(M_u\) = factored moment (kip-in), \(\phi = 0.9\), \(f_y\) = 60,000 psi, \(d\) = 22 in., \(a\) = depth of equivalent stress block (in). Worked: As = 471.13×12,000 / [0.9×60,000×(22−6.09/2)] = 5.52 in². (Standard strength-design relation; not tied to a single numbered ACI equation in the source.)
