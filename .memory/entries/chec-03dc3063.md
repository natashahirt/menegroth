**Proof runner** `scripts/runners/dual_highrise_drift_proof.jl`: 30-story perimeter steel moment frame (1080 sized members) around a stiff central core (inner 2×2 columns, I=0.015 m⁴, background), rigid-link-free (floor beams give diaphragm coupling), combined gravity+triangular lateral case. Drift constraints = elastic inter-story drift at the corner column line, limit δxe = Δa·Ie/Cd = 0.020·4·1/5.5 = 0.0145 m (ASCE 7-22 §12.12 + SMF Cd=5.5, Table 12.2-1 D.9). Strength floor = transparent flexural proxy I_floor=(d/2)/(φFy)·M (displacement-dependent → exercises real strength↔drift coupling).

**Result**: max drift ratio 4.93 → 0.967 (all stories under limit), strength floor met, converged in 5 outer iterations (~8-10 s), MF steel volume 48.9 → 69.0 m³ (×1.41).
