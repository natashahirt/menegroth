User accepted the recommended rectilinear-hoop arrangement on 2026-11-11.

Where an element is required:
- Two curtains of longitudinal bars along the §18.10.6.4(a) length, spaced at no more than hx from §18.10.6.4(f): the lesser of 14 in. and two-thirds of the boundary thickness (corpus: aci-318-19, page 328).
- One rectilinear hoop around the element. When the element is longer than twice its thickness, overlapping hoops. Each leg is at most twice the thickness, and adjacent hoops overlap by at least the lesser of 6 in. and two-thirds of the thickness.
- A crosstie with a seismic hook at each end across the thickness at every longitudinal bar that is not at a hoop corner. This is conservative relative to §18.7.5.2(d) via §25.7.2.3, which has not been read yet. Supporting every bar makes hx equal the bar spacing.
- Hoop and crosstie size from §25.7.2.2, to be read before the bars are placed. Vertical spacing is the least of one-third of the least boundary dimension (§18.10.6.4(e)), the rest of §18.7.5.3, and Table 18.10.6.5(b), then tightened so the hoop area meets Table 18.10.6.4(g).
- Over the distance above and below the critical section, crossties with seismic hooks at each end on the web vertical bars, at no more than 12 in. vertically (§18.10.6.4(i)). That 12 in. is the tie spacing, not the web-bar spacing.

Not placed in the floor-concrete and opening-edge pass.
