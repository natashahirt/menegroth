# Maximum stirrup spacing 0.7dv or 600 mm (§11.3.8.1; threshold §11.3.8.3)

Maximum stirrup spacing is the smaller of 0.7dv and 600 mm per CSA A23.3-14 §11.3.8.1 (governed here to 350 mm; 10M @ 250 mm used), with the low-shear threshold check per §11.3.8.3.
