# Deflection shown for illustration despite waiver; spBeam tool

Although the minimum-depth requirement waives deflection computations, immediate and long-term deflections are computed for illustration and comparison with spBeam, a software program for analysis, design, and investigation of beams and one-way slab systems per ACI 318-14 and CSA A23.3-14.
