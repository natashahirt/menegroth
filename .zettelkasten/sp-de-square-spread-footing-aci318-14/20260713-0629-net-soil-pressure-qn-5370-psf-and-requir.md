# Net soil pressure qn = 5370 psf and required area

With an assumed 32 in. thickness, net soil pressure = 5370 psf, giving a required footing plan area of ~125 ft^2 (~11.18 ft square); an 11 ft 2 in. square by 32 in. thick footing is selected.
