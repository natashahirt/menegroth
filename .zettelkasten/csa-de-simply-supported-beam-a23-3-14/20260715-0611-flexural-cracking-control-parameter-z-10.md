# Flexural cracking control parameter z (10.6.1)

\( z = f_s\,(d_c\,A)^{1/3} \); \(f_s\) steel service stress (MPa), \(d_c\) cover to bar centroid (mm), \(A\) effective tension area per bar (mm²).
