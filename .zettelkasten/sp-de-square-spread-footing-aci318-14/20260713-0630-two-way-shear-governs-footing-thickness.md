# Two-way shear governs footing thickness (ACI 318-14 §22.6.2.1)

Spread-footing thickness is commonly governed by two-way (punching) shear; the average effective depth is taken as the average of the effective depths in the two orthogonal directions, per ACI 318-14 §22.6.2.1.
