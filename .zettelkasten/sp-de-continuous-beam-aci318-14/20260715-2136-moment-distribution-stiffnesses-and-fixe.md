# Moment-distribution stiffnesses and fixed-end moment

Beam flexural stiffness Kb = 4·Ec·Ib/Lb = 4×(3834.3×40,634)/(40×12) = 1,298,357 kip-in. Column stiffnesses: top-ext 1,310,872; bottom-ext 1,111,633; top-int 3,397,780; bottom-int 2,881,353 kip-in. Fixed-end moment FEM = wu·Lb²/12 = 3.48×40²/12 = 464.08 kip-ft.
