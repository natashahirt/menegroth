# Immediate deflection procedure and cantilever fr rule (9.8.2.3 / Eq. 9.1)

Immediate deflection uses elastic equations with the effective moment of inertia Ie of Eq. 9.1; for the deflection check the modulus of rupture fr is taken as half of Eq. 8.3 per CSA A23.3-14 (9.8.2.3).
