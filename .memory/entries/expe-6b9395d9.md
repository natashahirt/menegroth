Built the production orbit core `StructuralSynthesizer/src/analyze/members/members/symmetry.jl` (`skeleton_automorphisms`, `member_group_orbits`) — ports the validated prototype to operate on `struc.skeleton` (vertices + `edge_indices`, role-preserving over all `groups_edges`). Read-only diagnostic `sdf_e0c0_orbit_diag.jl` designs e0c0 (waffle, 12 iters) and inspects the converged state. NO sizer changed.

**Orbit detector works:** 120 beam member-groups -> 32 orbits (3.75x collapse); correctly groups the hub girders (the original beam-11/44 case: T20x36-bf20 vs bf44) into orbits of 4. 32 multi-member orbits, **14 with mismatched installed sections = the chirality**.

**KEY FINDING — there are (at least) TWO chirality mechanisms, not one:**
1. **Demand/threshold amplification** (dominant, large effect). Chiral orbits show LARGE converged demand spread (35-75%): once a tiny seed splits the section, stiffness feedback diverges the demands. e.g. T20x36-bf20 vs bf44 (35%), T10x16-bf26 vs bf10 (74%), T10x20-bf10 vs bf26 (75%). The spread at convergence is the AMPLIFIED RESULT, not the seed (prior work put the pass-0 seed at ~0.01-0.05%). Fixable by **per-iteration orbit-max demand** — symmetrizing demand every pass prevents the amplification from ever starting.
2. **Flange-width path-dependence** (cosmetic, ~1cm). Several mismatched orbits have demand_spread = **0%** yet split sections by effective flange width (bf23 vs bf24). Force demand is identical; the T-beam effective-flange-width calc (tributary/geometry) is itself path-dependent. **Orbit-max on FORCE demand does NOT fix these** — need to also orbit-symmetrize the flange width (bf).

**Implication for the decoupled fix:** orbit-max demand alone is NECESSARY but NOT SUFFICIENT. The complete decoupled fix symmetrizes BOTH inputs to sizing across each orbit, order-independently: (a) the force-demand envelope (per iteration, to stop amplification) and (b) the effective flange width. Both are 'demand-side' (do not broadcast sections), so the verifier/broadcast machinery is still deferred. Reinforces caution: a single demand symmetrization would leave a residual cosmetic bf chirality.
