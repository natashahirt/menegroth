# Moment redistribution reduction limit — 1000ε_t up to 20 percent (§6.6.5.3)

ACI 318-14 permits reducing elastically-computed factored moments at sections of maximum negative or positive moment in any span of continuous flexural members, for any assumed loading arrangement, by a percentage equal to 1000ε_t, capped at 20 percent.
