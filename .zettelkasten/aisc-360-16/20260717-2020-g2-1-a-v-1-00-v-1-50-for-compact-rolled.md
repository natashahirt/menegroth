# G2.1(a) φv = 1.00, Ωv = 1.50 for Compact Rolled Webs

For compact rolled I-shaped webs meeting G2.1(a), φv = 1.00 (LRFD) and Ωv = 1.50 (ASD), replacing the default G1 factors.
