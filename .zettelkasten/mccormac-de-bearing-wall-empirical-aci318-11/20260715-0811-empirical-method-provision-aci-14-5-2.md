# ACI empirical bearing-wall provision (§14.5.2 → §11.5.3.1)

Load-bearing walls with solid rectangular cross sections may be designed either
as columns under axial load + bending (rational method, code §14.4) or by the
empirical method of code §14.5. The empirical method (§14.5.2) provides a
single closed-form design axial strength for solid rectangular walls and is the
direct predecessor of ACI 318-19 §11.5.3.1 (same equation, renumbered). The
example follows PCA *Notes on ACI 318-08* pp. 21-17 to 21-18.
