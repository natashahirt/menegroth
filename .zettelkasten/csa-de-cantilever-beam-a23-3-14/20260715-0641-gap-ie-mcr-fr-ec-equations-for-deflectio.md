# GAP — Ie / Mcr / fr / Ec equations for deflection (Eq. 9.1, 9.2, 8.3, 8.6.2.2)

Deflection relies on Ie (Eq. 9.1), cracking moment Mcr (Eq. 9.2), modulus of rupture fr (Eq. 8.3), and modulus of elasticity Ec (8.6.2.2); all equation OCRs are garbled and must not be transcribed. Legible numeric results: Ec = 25,967.93 MPa, Mcr = 39.38 kN·m, Icr = 3.9002×10⁹ mm⁴, Ie(total) = 3.9123×10⁹ mm⁴.
