# Parameters Myc, Rpc, Sxc for Eq. F4-1

Myc = Fy Sxc = yield moment in the compression flange (kip-in.); Rpc = web plastification factor per Section F4.2(c)(6) [printed as F4.2(b)(6) in the body text]; Sxc = elastic section modulus referred to the compression flange (in.^3).
