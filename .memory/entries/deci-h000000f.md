# Decision - split `design` from member structs (2026-01-29)

**Context.** Commit `68a1f98f` "refactor structs to have separate design struct" split the mutable **design result** (chosen section, checks, utilization) away from the member/analysis definition, alongside implementing HSS sections and adding tests.

**Rationale.** Decoupling *what a member is* from *what the sizer decided for it* lets the same member be re-sized/compared across runs, keeps optimizer output cleanly separable, and underpins the later trace/experiment machinery (Epoch 7) and cross-floor comparison. Complements the sibling decision to make a slab's load-distribution method an inherited trait (`1136c461`).
