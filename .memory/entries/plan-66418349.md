Punching near openings runs in plan axes for every column shape.

Column centre is the skeleton vertex plus structural_offset. Sections are built there: a rectangle rotated by the column angle for interior, edge, and orthogonal corners; circular_critical_section for round columns; corner_critical_section with the real angle for re-entrant and skewed corners. Openings are only translated to that centre. Primary and secondary unbalanced moments are turned into plan-axis components by the slab span angle, shifted to the section centroid, and floored at zero (ACI 318-11 R11.11.7.2). When no opening reaches, orthogonal columns keep the closed form.

The flat-slab column-strip trigger uses 0.25·min(l1,l2) of the panels framing that column (ACI 318-11 §13.2.1). The drop-edge section uses the drop panel as the reaction area: rays from the drop centroid, 10h from the drop outline, h the slab thickness outside the drop (ACI 318-11 §11.11.6, §11.11.1.2). It stays direct shear. The optimizer receives the same opening arguments. Shearheads and §13.4.2.1 bar placement stay out of this change.
