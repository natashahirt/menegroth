# Design negative moments at face of support, ≤ 0.175ℓ from center (§8.11.6.1)

Negative moments for design are taken at the faces of supports but not at distances greater than 0.175ℓ from the centers of supports. Here the face offsets (1.00 ft interior, 0.83 ft exterior) are both < 0.175×40 = 7.00 ft, so face-of-support locations govern. Clause 8.11.6.1, page 10.
