# Column base contact-bearing tolerance (§M4.4)

AISC 360-16 permits a lack of contact bearing at column compression joints and base plates up to a gap of 1/16 in. (2 mm) regardless of splice type; larger gaps up to 1/4 in. (6 mm) require shimming, subject to engineering evaluation.
