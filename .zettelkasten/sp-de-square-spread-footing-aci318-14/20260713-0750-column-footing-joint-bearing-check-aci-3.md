# Column-footing joint bearing check (ACI 318-14 Table 22.8.3.2)

The column-footing joint is checked for maximum bearing at the top of the footing and allowable bearing at the base of the column, per ACI 318-14 Table 22.8.3.2, with φ=0.65 for bearing.
