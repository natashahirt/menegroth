# F4 Scope — Other I-Shaped Members, Compact/Noncompact Webs

Section F4 applies to doubly symmetric I-shaped members bent about their major axis with noncompact webs, and to singly symmetric I-shaped members with webs attached at flange mid-width, with compact or noncompact webs, as defined in Section B4.1 for flexure.
