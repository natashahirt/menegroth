# Required stirrup shear strength Vs = Vu/φ − Vc/φ

Nominal shear to be carried by stirrups. LaTeX: \( V_s = V_n - V_c = \dfrac{V_u}{\phi} - \dfrac{V_c}{\phi} \). Symbols: \(V_n\) = nominal shear, \(V_u\) = factored shear at d, \(V_c\) = concrete shear, \(\phi = 0.75\). Worked: Vs = 65.82/0.75 − 33.39/0.75 = 43.24 kips.
