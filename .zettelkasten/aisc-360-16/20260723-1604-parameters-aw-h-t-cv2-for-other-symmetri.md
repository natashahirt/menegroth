# Parameters Aw, h, t, Cv2 for Other Symmetric Shapes (G4)

Aw = sum of dtw for each web; Cv2 per G2.2 with h/tw=h/t and kv=5; h = clear distance between flanges (welded) or fastener lines (bolted); t = web thickness per B4.2.
