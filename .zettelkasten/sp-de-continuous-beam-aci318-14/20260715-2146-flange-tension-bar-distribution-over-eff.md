# Flange-tension bar distribution over effective flange width ≤ ℓn/10 (§24.3.2.1)

Where T-beam flanges are in tension, part of the bonded flexural tension reinforcement must be distributed over the effective flange width but not wider than ℓn/10 (ℓn = clear span face-to-face of supports). Here bmin = min(beff, ℓn/10) = 45.8 in. Clause 24.3.2.1, page 12.
