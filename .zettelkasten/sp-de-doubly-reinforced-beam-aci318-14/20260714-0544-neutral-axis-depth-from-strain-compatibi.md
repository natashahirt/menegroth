# Neutral-axis depth from strain compatibility (§22.2.2.4.2)

The neutral-axis depth c at the tension-controlled limit is found from strain compatibility between εcu at the extreme compression fiber and the tensile strain at dt, per ACI 318-14 §22.2.2.4.2.
