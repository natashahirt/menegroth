## Decision
`_steel_rib_composite` (scripts/runners/sdf_nova_topology_systems.jl) no longer uses `TwoWayOptions` + composite steel (which routed through the story shell and caused the 10.5 h runaway / group-8 infeasibility, find-e8f16711). It now uses the one-way composite-deck path:
- floor = CompositeDeckOptions(deck_profile="3VLI", fill_material=DECK_FILL, total_depth=6.5in, defl 1/360 & 1/240) — floor_symbol :composite_deck, which `_story_shell_design_levels` excludes (only :one_way/:two_way go to the shell).
- beams = SteelBeamOptions(composite=true, composite_slab_type=:solid, composite_shored=true, defl 1/360 & 1/240).

Distinguished from :steel_deck by a deeper SOLID structural fill (6.5 in total vs steel_deck's minimum), a solid composite flange, and SHORED construction (skips the pre-cure bare-steel check).

## Validation (topology/r2c1, max_iterations=4)
Solved in **1423.6 s (~24 min)** vs the prior 37,829 s (10.5 h) failure. Critical = Beam 5 deflection_ll at ratio ~1.0 (deflection-governed, as expected for a heavy 62 psf composite floor) — a tractable design, not an infeasibility. Note: 6.5 in solid fill is heavy; could drop to 5.0 in if a lighter floor is wanted.
