# Limits of applicability for HSS-to-HSS truss connections (Tables K3.1A, K3.2A)

AISC 360-16 Tables K3.1A/K3.2A bound the truss-connection strength equations by width ratio (0.2 < D_b/D ≤ 1.0 for T-/Y-/cross-/overlapped K-connections; 0.4 < D_b/D ≤ 1.0 for gapped K-connections), chord and branch wall slenderness, gap, overlap (25% ≤ O_v ≤ 100%), and eccentricity limits.
