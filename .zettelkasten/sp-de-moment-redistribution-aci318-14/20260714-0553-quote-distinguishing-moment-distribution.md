# Quote: distinguishing moment distribution vs redistribution

It is important in this example to distinguish between the two terms: moment distribution and moment
redistribution. Moment distribution is a structural analysis method for statically indeterminate beams and
frames, while moment redistribution refers to the behavior of statically indeterminate structures that are not
completely elastic, but have some reserve plastic capacity.
