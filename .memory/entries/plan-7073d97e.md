Cleanup and speed plan for the wall stage after the shared rigid/flexible force path (plan-a6412144, expe-80ebb8bf). Nothing is broken. One reporting gap and one repeated full-model solve are the work. Two ASCE questions stay unanswered and are not part of this implementation.

## Implement, in order

1. **Report unchecked flexible drift as a failure.** A flexible lateral run now sizes walls first-order, but seismic story drift and P-delta come back empty. An empty drift list can read as passing. Until drift is defined for a flexible floor, the ordinary-wall result must say drift was not checked and must not pass. Gravity-only designs stay empty and passing: they have no earthquake. Do not invent a drift number.
2. **Solve each lateral case once.** `wind_pier_actions` and `seismic_strength_pier_actions` both call `_story_case_pier_actions`, which calls `Asap.solve` per pier. With rigid links each call rebuilds and refactors the reduced stiffness (`external/Asap/src/Elements/rigidlink.jl`). `_first_order_case_reports` solves the same loads again for opening segments. Keep the solved displacements on `WindStoryAnalysis` and on the ELF strength cases, and have every pier and every segment read them. Combined earthquake effects are linear sums of the stored cases. This is reuse inside one stage run. It is not the cross-run cache in `wall_cache_contract.jl`. For a 12-pier building this removes about 26 full-model factorizations.
3. **Merge the duplicated stage machinery.** The gravity pier loop in `_design_gravity_walls!` and the lateral pier loop repeat the same steps (skip pierced walls, assembly, clear length, slab moment, reinforcement, raised count, max ratios). `_build_gravity_second_order` and `_build_story_second_order` differ only by the extra lateral batch. Master lookup is written three times (`_elf_masters`, the copy in `_wind_levels`, `_gravity_diaphragm_geometry`). ELF and wind each build story levels. Three 1e-4 m vertical tolerances (`WALL_CUT_ZTOL_M`, `_WALL_VERTEX_Z_TOL_M`, `_STORY_FLOOR_Z_TOL_M`) should be one constant.
4. **Split `ordinary_wall_stage.jl` (1,489 lines).** Move drift helpers into `story_demands.jl` and the opening-segment checks into their own file. The stage file should read as the stage. Fix the `wind_pier_actions` docstring, which says the story analysis keeps master displacements only, once step 2 stores the displacements.

Steps 2–4 must not change numerical results. The existing wall, ELF, wind, and diaphragm tests are the check.

## Settled, docstring only

Dropping the 5% accidental torsion on a flexible floor is correct. ASCE 7-22 §12.8.4.2.1 (p. 187, asce-7-22) applies accidental torsion only where diaphragms are not flexible, and C12.8.4.1 (p. 727) assumes no computed torsion. Update the docstrings that still call this a later decision. Do not add a couple.

## Not in this plan — needs a decision before any calculation changes

- **Flexible drift measurement.** §12.8.6.5 (p. 188) measures drift at the center of mass, and diaphragm deformation may be neglected. A flexible floor has no single representative point. Options left open: the largest drift at any wall or column top, or a projected center of mass.
- **Flexible force split.** §12.8.4.1 (p. 187) says forces account for the position and distribution of the masses supported. The current split is by plan area. That matches only when weight per area is uniform. Switching the split from area to seismic weight is a later change, not this plan.

## Still deferred, already tracked elsewhere

Deformation compatibility for SDC D/E/F (§12.12.4 / ACI §18.14), diaphragm design, rebar detailing around openings, stirrups for wall segments that act as columns, general strut-and-tie for multi-panel horizontal pieces, and the two reminder tests (AISC §F4, column optimizer 0.01 band).
