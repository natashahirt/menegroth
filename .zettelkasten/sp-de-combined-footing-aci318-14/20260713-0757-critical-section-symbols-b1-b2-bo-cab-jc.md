# Critical-section symbols b1, b2, bo, cAB, Jc, γf, γv

The punching-shear calculation defines b1 (critical-section dimension parallel to span), b2 (perpendicular dimension), perimeter length bo, centroidal-axis location cAB, polar moment Jc, and moment-transfer fractions γf and γv.
