Source: Wilson & Habibullah (1987), Earthquake Spectra 3(2) 289–298, added to corpus as wilson-habibullah-1987-pdelta (research/lateral). It is an image-only scan; the text was transcribed from page renders because tesseract is not installed (brew build was still compiling cmake).

Decisions (user-confirmed 2026-10-30):
- Eq. 12 prints the torsion entries as 'r'. Eq. 11, Eq. 17, and units require r_i². Implement r_i². Erratum recorded in the manifest note.
- Torsion basis: the paper's 'more exact form' Σ p_m r_m², using the factored vertical forces of the members crossing each story cut (not the floor-mass radius of gyration). Each force is placed through the rigid-floor kinematics of the top and bottom masters, which carries the Eq. 13 centroid transformation automatically.

Implementation (Steps 1–2):
- Asap: `solve(model, cases, links; geometric_stiffness=K_G)` adds a signed K_G before the rigid-link reduction and refuses terms on tied slave DOFs. Test (e) in test_rigidlink.jl: sway and twist match u0/(1−θ) to 1e-9 (Sherman–Morrison exact), base shear = H + WΔ/h.
- Synthesizer `analyze/lateral/story_pdelta.jl`: `StoryAxialProfile`, `story_axial_profiles` (same cut as check_story_cut_equilibrium; base must be fixed in plan), `story_geometric_stiffness`. Tests: member form equals −AᵀL'A of Eqs. 12–14 with offset masters and a tension member; gravity cut then lateral solve gives Δ0/(1−θ) with θ≈0.135.

Not yet done: Step 3 (per-combination factored-gravity K_G and story-cut equilibrium counting P·Δ/h), Step 4 (wire into wall stage for wind/gravity/seismic per ACI 318-19 §6.6.4 with the §6.2.5.3 1.4 cap; ASCE §12.8.7 stays the drift/θmax gate). Asap submodule edits are uncommitted.
