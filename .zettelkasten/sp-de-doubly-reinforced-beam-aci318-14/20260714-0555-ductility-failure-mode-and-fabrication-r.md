# Ductility, failure-mode, and fabrication rationale for compression steel

Compression reinforcement shifts the failure mode from brittle (compression) to ductile (tension), increases ductility useful for seismic/moment-redistribution cases, and is also used for fabrication (anchoring stirrups).
