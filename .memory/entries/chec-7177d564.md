## What

`drift_optimality_resize_multi(C, s, w, s_min, δ_allow) -> MultiDriftOptimalityResult` in `StructuralSizer/src/optimize/drift_optimality.jl`. Generalizes the single-constraint OC resizer to m simultaneous drift limits (per-story inter-story drift under seismic §12.12 + total-height wind serviceability ~H/400). `C` is the n×m participation matrix (column j sums to current δ_j). Wind and multi-story seismic are just extra columns with their own δ_allow — one machinery covers both.

## Algorithm — Newton dual over an active set (NOT the first-order recursion)
- KKT: s_e(λ) = sqrt((Σ_j λ_j γ_{e,j})/w_e), γ_{e,j}=C[e,j]·s_e. Dual φ(λ) concave; gradient = constraint residual g_j=δ_j(λ)−δ_allow_j; Hessian H_{jk}=−Σ_{e off floor} γ_{e,j}γ_{e,k}/(2 w_e s_e^3).
- Solve g_A=0 by damped Newton (Δ=−H\g, α-backtrack keeping λ≥0, ‖g‖ decrease). Active set: warm-start violated limits from single-constraint λ, drop limits whose λ→0 (slack), add inactive limits that become violated. Strength floors via max(s_min,·), excluded from H.
- Reduces to the single-constraint closed form at m=1.

## Why Newton, not the Khot/Berke multiplicative recursion
Traced the first-order recursion on a near-degenerate coupled 3-member/2-limit case (participation [1.2,0.6,0.4] vs [1.0,0.7,0.2]): r crawled 0.70→0.9987 over 500 iters without converging (both limits genuinely bind). Newton nails it in **5 iters**, both tight r=[1.0,1.0], λ=[0.0512,0.0106]. Near-degenerate coupled story-drift limits are the norm in tall buildings, so the first-order zig-zag would bite in production. Replaced it wholesale per the general/clean-over-patch preference — dropped η/move_limit/kkt_tol knobs; kwargs now just max_iters (Newton cap/pass) + tol (relative residual, 1e-8).
