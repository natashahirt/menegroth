## Bug
`FEMLateralEvaluator` ignored `StoryLoad.ex/ey` and put the story force on the floor master node. In `:rigid` mode the master is the lowest-plan wall node (a corner, e.g. (-5,-2)), so the load was 2 m off the centre: symmetric 4-wall core, pure Fx -> torsion 1.22, W1/W2 = 1.50, and `story_disp` was read at that corner. `:penalty_shell` was right only because its master is the wall-node centroid. Production building models use `model_builder.jl`'s own rigid diaphragm and were not affected; this evaluator is the S3 validation tier.

## Fix (`StructuralSizer/src/walls/fem_evaluator.jl`)
- Force on the master plus the transfer couple Mz = (ex-mx)Fy - (ey-my)Fx, applied as equal and opposite in-plane nodal forces on the two farthest-apart floor wall nodes (`_couple_forces`). Nodal forces, not a theta-z moment, because the penalty master hangs on drilling DOFs only.
- Floor displacement carried rigidly from the master to the plan origin: ux0 = ux_m + theta*my, uy0 = uy_m - theta*mx. The torsion metric and drift assume the origin.
- No ASCE 7 accidental eccentricity is added in this tier (unchanged).

## Evidence
Symmetric core, mesh_div=3, load at (0,1): penalty vs rigid twist -3.26e-7 vs -3.23e-7, torsion 1.094 vs 1.105, W1/W2 0.810 vs 0.817; penalty master theta matches a least-squares rigid fit of the floor nodes within 0.5%. Base reactions balance Fx and +1e5 N*m exactly in both modes (new regression testset in `test_fem_rigid_diaphragm.jl`).

## S3 test tolerance failure (separate cause)
`test_walls.jl` S3 torsion 1.00186 vs atol 1e-3: all four panels ran the same way, so the triangle diagonals made the meshed core asymmetric (1.0032 / 1.0019 / 1.0010 at mesh_div 1/3/6). Panels 2 and 4 now run opposite -> exactly 1.0; check tightened to 1e-6. Same fixture fix in `StructuralSynthesizer/test/report_generators/test_walls_report.jl`.
