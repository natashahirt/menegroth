# Factored reaction Pu = 64.8 k and minimum thickness 7.68 in

Minimum thickness (§14.5.3.1): `(1/25)(12 in/ft × 16 ft) = 7.68 in.` governs
over the 4 in. floor ⇒ **try h = 8 in.** Factored beam reaction (ACI load
combination): `Pu = 1.2(30 k) + 1.6(18 k) = 64.8 k`.
