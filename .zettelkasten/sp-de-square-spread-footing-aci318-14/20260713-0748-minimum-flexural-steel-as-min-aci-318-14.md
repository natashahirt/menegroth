# Minimum flexural steel As,min (ACI 318-14 §7.6.1.1)

Minimum footing reinforcement per ACI 318-14 §7.6.1.1 is the greater of 0.0018×(60,000/fy) or 0.0014 times the gross section, giving As,min=7.72 in.^2, less than the required As.
