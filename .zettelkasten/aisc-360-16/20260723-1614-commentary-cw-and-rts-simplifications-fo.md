# Commentary — Cw and rts Simplifications for Rectangular-Flange I-Shapes

A User Note gives, for doubly symmetric I-shapes with rectangular flanges, $C_w = I_y h_o^2/4$ so that $r_{ts}^2 = I_y h_o/(2 S_x)$, and rts may be conservatively approximated as the compression flange's radius of gyration plus one-sixth of the web.
