# Flange-tension reinforcement distribution over overhang (§10.5.3.1)

Where flanges are in tension, part of the tension reinforcement is distributed over an overhang width of 1/20 of span (or Clause 10.3 width, whichever smaller), with area ≥ 0.004 times the gross overhanging-flange area, per CSA A23.3-14 §10.5.3.1.
