# Empirical load-bearing wall worked-example scope (McCormac 9e, Ex. 18.1)

McCormac & Brown, *Design of Reinforced Concrete* 9th ed., §18.3 + Example 18.1:
design of a solid rectangular load-bearing concrete wall by the ACI **empirical
design method** (ACI 318 §14.5.2, Eq. 14-1). The wall supports precast roof
beams at 7 ft on center; the example computes minimum thickness, the effective
horizontal length per concentrated load, and the design axial strength φPnw,
then compares it to the factored beam reaction. Serves as an independent
numeric known-answer oracle for `check_wall_bearing_empirical` /
`wall_empirical_axial_capacity_si` (ACI 318-19 §11.5.3.1 is the renumbered,
formula-identical successor of Eq. 14-1).
