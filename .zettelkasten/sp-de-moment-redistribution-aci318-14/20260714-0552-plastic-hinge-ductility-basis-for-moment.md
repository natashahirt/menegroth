# Plastic-hinge ductility basis for moment redistribution (R6.6.5)

Redistribution depends on adequate ductility in plastic-hinge regions that form at maximum-moment sections and shift the elastic moment diagram, typically reducing maximum negative support moments and increasing positive span moments; because negative and positive maxima arise under different load arrangements, narrowing the envelope can economize reinforcement.
