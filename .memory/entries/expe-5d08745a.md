Only the story-shell model failed Cholesky (2350 nodes, 14080 free DOFs) on the 54x42 ft 3x3 auto-framing floor. Free-DOF diag reached 1e51 on ux of 8 nodes vs median 7.5e8; no frame piece had |K|>1e15. Cause: 3 zero-area ShellTri3 'story' triangles, each three consecutive points of a beam line where it meets the outline at a column, e.g. (0,8.540000000000001),(0.4581,8.54),(0.9162,8.54). _drop_colinear removed boundary vertices at line/outline junctions, Asap seeded its own boundary points (1-ulp noise), the beam endpoint deduped onto the noisy seed, and the exact-predicate CDT kept the sliver.

Fix: StructuralSizer story_shell meshes with `_with_line_points_on_loop(outer/holes, lines)` (every constrained-line point lying on the outline becomes an exact vertex); returned `boundary` unchanged. Regression test in StructuralSizer/test/slabs/test_story_shell.jl (synthetic 3x3 54x42 ft: former path 20 flat triangles, now 0). StructuralSizer story-shell tests 41/41, StructuralSynthesizer story-shell tests 250/250.

After fix the section trace has no SPD warnings and the pass-1 493 kip-ft hogging spike is gone (demands now < ~35 kip-ft), but sizing still does not settle: 30-115 of 147 beams change per pass. Two behaviours: load attraction growth (W4X13 -> W8X24 -> W10X45 -> W10X54 -> W12X58 as Mu rises 4 -> 28 kip-ft) and shape swapping (W8X35 <-> W10X39). Sections are far heavier than the listed Mu needs (W8X31 for Mu- ~10 kip-ft), suggesting hogging LTB with long Lb controls. Log: logs/diag_story_shell_sections_after_mesh_fix.log.
