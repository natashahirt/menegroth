# Limits of applicability for plate-to-round-HSS connections (Table K2.1A)

AISC 360-16 Table K2.1A bounds the plate-to-round-HSS design equations to material strength F_y ≤ 52 ksi (360 MPa) with F_y/F_u ≤ 0.8 (ASTM A500 Grade C acceptable), plus width-ratio and wall-slenderness limits.
