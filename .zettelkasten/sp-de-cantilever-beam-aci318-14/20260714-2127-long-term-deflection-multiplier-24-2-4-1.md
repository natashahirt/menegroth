# Long-Term Deflection Multiplier (§24.2.4.1.1, Table 24.2.4.1.3)

Additional long-term deflection from creep and shrinkage Δcs = λΔ·(Δsust)Inst, with λΔ = ξ/(1+50ρ′); ξ = 2 for sustained duration ≥ 60 months and ρ′ = 0 conservatively, giving λΔ = 2.
