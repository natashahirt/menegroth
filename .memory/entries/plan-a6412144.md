## Force path (do this first)

Today there are two distributions. Rigid: `_elf_loads` and `_wind_loads` put Fx, Fy, and Mz on the rigid-diaphragm master. Flexible: `distribute_flexible_diaphragm_story_force` splits (Fx, Fy) by area and writes nothing. `to_asap!` stores the areas. `elf_story_analysis`, `wind_story_analysis`, and `design_ordinary_walls!` throw when `asap_rigid_links` is empty. Gravity can already run without those links; it stays first-order.

One load builder should own both. Input is one story force (Fx, Fy) and, when rigid, the accidental torsion. Output is `Vector{Asap.AbstractLoad}`.

- Rigid: keep the present master force and master moment. Drift authority stays the rigid link.
- Flexible: no master. For each column and each wall of the story below that floor, apply `F * A / ΣA` as a horizontal `NodeForce` at the nodes of that floor. A column uses `column_tributary_area`. A wall uses `wall_tributary_area` (the stored complement). Zero area is kept and receives no force. An empty list, a missing area, or a non-positive total still throws. A strut still throws (deci-b196a46b). Do not invent a share for a brace.
- Accidental torsion: it is a moment on the rigid master. A flexible diaphragm has no master. Do not paste that moment onto a wall node. Do not invent an equivalent couple from the eccentricity. Record that the 5% accidental eccentricity is unused on `:flexible` until a later decision. The rigid office is unaffected.
- Where the wall force is applied: the wall-shell nodes at that floor elevation, in the plane of the wall, using `wall_edge_nodal_forces` so the shape functions of the edge take the force. Do not dump the whole share on one arbitrary node. A column force goes to the column node at that floor.
- `to_asap!` still does not apply a story force. The analysis functions do, after tributaries exist.
- Semirigid (`:shell`) stays out of this slice. It is a different stiffness, not a tributary split.

Then lift the empty-`asap_rigid_links` throw in the ordinary-wall lateral stage, in `elf_story_analysis`, and in `wind_story_analysis`, only after those functions call the shared builder. Gravity second-order still requires rigid masters (`story_axial_profiles`). A flexible lateral run stays first-order. Do not apply §6.6.4.6.

Tests, in order: the existing 40-split-1:3 unit; a walls-only office with columns removed, every wall force sums to the story force; the 10×6 office with columns, the ground wall at y=0 keeps 30 m² and the upper walls that have an empty complement receive zero; a strut still throws; `design_ordinary_walls!` on that flexible office runs and does not ask for rigid masters.

## Openings bookkeeping (do with the force path, not after a new truss)

The horizontal loop in `_design_pierced_segments!` copies reinforcement only on the `:stm` branch. The `:beam` branch must copy as well, or a lintel that raises bars does not write them back onto the wall. Pull the shared start/end-action collection out of the two branches so the beam and the deep beam see the same cuts.

Document the deep-beam idealizations in one place: couple arm = overall depth h; one corner-to-corner strut; strut width ws = h·cos θ; node face = strut area; tie steel = ρt above 0.0025; §9.9.1.1(b), §23.5.2 spacing, and §23.8 development are unevaluated. Do not widen the truss. Do not validate against the StructurePoint transfer girder in this slice (ACI 318-14 βs table differs).

Leave unevaluated, and do not pretend they are done: §11.7.5.1 opening bars, column stirrup sizing (shear above φVc stays withheld), §6.6.4.6, a general k>1 truss.
