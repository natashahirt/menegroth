Probe (scripts/runners/wall_inplane_pdelta_probe.jl): cantilever wall 6 m x 12 m x 0.3 m, E = 0.35 x 25 GPa, out-of-plane translation fixed, top P and H chosen for theta = 0.10. Asap.solve_pdelta! ratio for the shell wall = 1.0000. The same wall as a vertical frame stick: ratio 1.126. Cause: local_geometric_stiffness(ShellTri3) only fills the out-of-plane w DOFs (plate buckling), so the in-plane sway P-delta of a wall is absent. Adding rigid links to solve_pdelta! would therefore capture only the gravity columns' P-delta and under-predict the wall building.

ACI 318-19 (corpus aci-318-19): 11.4.1.3 (PDF p.169) wall slenderness effects shall be calculated per 6.6.4, 6.7, or 6.8, so it applies to wind combinations, not only seismic. 6.6.4.3(b) and Eq. 6.6.4.4.1 (p.81): nonsway if Q = sum(Pu) Delta_o / (Vus lc) <= 0.05. 6.6.4.6.2 (p.83): delta_s = 1/(1-Q) >= 1; if delta_s > 1.5 only (b) or (c) second-order elastic analysis. 6.2.5.3 (p.72): Mu with second-order effects shall not exceed 1.4 Mu first-order. 6.7.2.1.1 (p.87): elastic second-order may use 6.6.3.1 properties. 6.2.5.1 (p.70) neglect-slenderness limits are written for columns and are not listed in 11.4.1.3 (open question for walls). Current wind wall checks apply no second-order effect.

ASCE 7-22 12.8.7 (p.188): with an automated P-delta analysis, Eq. 12.8-19 must still be satisfied; theta from the P-delta results may be divided by (1 + theta) first.
