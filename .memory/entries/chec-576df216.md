Beam.level is the support rank of the beam's line, written by assign_framing_levels! after joists exist.

A line of collinear beams with two or more column, wall, or :support vertices is level 0, even when drawn as several members. Any other line is one level above the higher of its two ends. A free tip does not raise the level. Same-level crossings support neither beam, so the span runs to the next lower line or column. ENGINEERING JUDGMENT: no code says which crossing beam supports the other.

Service deflection is the downward offset from the chord through those supports, divided by the support-to-support length. Each piece of a split line reports only the offset on itself. A cantilever is the tip relative to the support, and l is twice the cantilever (IBC 2021 Table 1604.3 footnote i, corpus ibc-2021, PDF p. 418). L/240 is live plus the whole cell.sdl. Slab self-weight and member self-weight are excluded. ENGINEERING JUDGMENT from AISC 360-16 Commentary L2 (corpus aisc-360-16, PDF p. 535): all cell.sdl stands in for dead applied after nonstructural attachment.

Generated FramingLevel members stay pinned. A skeleton beam keeps the freefree-to-fixed upgrade at an end that is not on a column or wall, at any support rank, because k_freefree cannot carry shear. Story-shell membership no longer compares Beam.level to the story index.
