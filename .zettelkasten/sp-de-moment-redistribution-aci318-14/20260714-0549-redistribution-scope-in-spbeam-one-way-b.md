# Redistribution scope in spBeam — one-way/beam systems, negative-moment reduction only

In spBeam the redistribution feature applies to one-way and beam systems only; only reduction (up to 20%) of negative moments over supports is considered, increase of negative moments (though code-permitted) is ignored, and static equilibrium is maintained.
