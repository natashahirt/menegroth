# Transverse-beam end support ≈ 2/3 of column-end moment (Table 6.5.2)

The ACI code approximates the moment at a transverse-beam end support as 2/3 of the moment at a column end support (ACI 318-14 Table 6.5.2), usable to back out the transverse-beam rotational stiffness by trial and error. For Grid 4 this yields ~204.6 kip-ft end moment vs ~304.6 kip-ft for Grid 3 (columns). Clause Table 6.5.2, page 42.
