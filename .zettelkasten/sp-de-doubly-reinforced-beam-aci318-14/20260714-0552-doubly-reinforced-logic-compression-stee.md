# Doubly reinforced logic — compression steel supplies the moment deficit

When the singly reinforced nominal moment is less than the required nominal moment, the deficit Mns′ = Mn,required − Mn,singly is carried by a compression/tension steel couple with lever arm (d − d′), sizing the compression force Cs = Mns′/(d − d′).
