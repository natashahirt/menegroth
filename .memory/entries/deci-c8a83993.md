A beam with no tributary load of its own still carries joist reactions through the bare-steel dead solve. When every dead load on the model is one tributary pressure, beam_pre_cure_shape returns that delivered moment in the existing split form: the area part scales with pressure, and the steel weight is a unit line load on this beam only. Two pressures, a dead load that is not a tributary load, or a beam the dead case does not reach, still keep the simple span.

Checked on a 6 m girder with one midspan joist. A 10 kPa load over 2 m on a 4 m joist delivers P = 40 kN. The pin-roller girder moment is PL/4 = 60 kN·m; fixed ends are PL/8 = 30 kN·m. Half the joist span halves the moment. Double the pressure doubles it. Each extra 4 in of slab adds the same construction moment, because C_VML grows with thickness and personnel and steel weight do not. Second call matches.
