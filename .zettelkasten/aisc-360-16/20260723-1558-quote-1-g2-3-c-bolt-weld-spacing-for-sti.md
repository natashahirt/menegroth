# Quote 1: G2.3(c) — Bolt/Weld Spacing for Stiffener Attachment

(c) Bolts connecting stiffeners to the girder web shall be spaced not more than 12 in.
(300 mm) on center. If intermittent fillet welds are used, the clear distance
between welds shall not be more than 16 times the web thickness nor more than
10 in. (250 mm).
