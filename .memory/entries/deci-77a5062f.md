SteelBeamOptions.composite=true in size_beams! now builds one PerStationComposite per beam group instead of the Chapter I boolean sentinel. Each beam keeps its per-side tributary profiles and the exact FEM moment field. For every candidate section the checker re-solves the sagging station that maximizes M⁺(s)/max(ϕMn(b_eff(s)), ϕMn_steel), with ΣQn = Cf(b_eff) (full composite). Slab spacings passed back into the scalar checker are 2× the already-capped half-widths, so get_b_eff is not capped a second time. Construction (§I3.1b) and hogging (§I3.2b) stay on the demand envelopes. Asr is not invented, so neg_moment stays false and hogging is Chapter F. Slab concrete is resolve_slab_concrete on the design path; thickness is composite_t_slab or the sized slab depth. Missing either throws. The stored headed stud (3/4 in × 5 in, Fu 65 ksi, Fy 51 ksi) does not enter ϕMn.
