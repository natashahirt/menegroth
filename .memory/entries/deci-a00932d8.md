## What & why

**Engineering review finding.** The vetted StructuralSizer AISC checker computes steel compression capacity **correctly** (`StructuralSizer.get_ϕPn` → AISC 360-16 §E3 flexural buckling Eq. E3-2/E3-3, §E4 torsional, §E7 effective area, φc=0.90; ϕPnc = min over strong/weak/torsional; Lc = Kx·L / Ky·L).
