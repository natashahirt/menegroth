Generating full-fidelity scenes (max_iterations=12, cache ON) for the three swept plans revealed that the earlier claim 'all three converge early, 6 vs 13 is nearly free' is WRONG in general:

- star/e0c0: CONVERGED by iter6. iter6 and iter12 give an identical installed design (critical Beam 115 flexure r=0.9946). For star, 6 == 12. Scene written (sdf_star_e0c0_waffle_sweep.mgscene, 498 s).
- grid/x1y1: did NOT converge by iter6. iter6 critical was Column 3 (pm_interaction) r=0.990; iter12 critical is Beam 56 (flexure) r=0.9932 — a genuinely different design — and runtime rose ~5x (306 s -> 1508 s). The iter6 log line 'Story shell and frame agree on the installed sections' does NOT mean the iterative member/shell loop reached a fixed point; the design still moves with more iterations. all_checks_pass stayed true. Scene written (sdf_grid_x1y1_waffle_sweep.mgscene).
- special/rhombus_8x12: ERRORS at iter12 with 'pinned section check made no progress' (pinned-section reconciliation stalls). At iter6 it passed (Beam 35 flexure r=0.997). So higher iteration counts can turn a passing design into a hard error for this geometry — likely the same non-convergence/oscillation family as the dense-waffle min-reinforcement issue (todo ribsteel). No scene written.

Implication: the T-beam cache exactness result (ON==OFF at a fixed iter count) is unaffected — that is about cache correctness. But iteration-count is a real design variable here, not a free fidelity knob, and the 'pinned section check made no progress' stall at iter12 on rhombus is a convergence bug worth its own investigation.
