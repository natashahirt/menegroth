Pier decisions for Table 12.2-1 rows A1 and B4 now state continuity from the wall stack and leave a single critical section unstated, so the route stays ACI 318-19 §18.10.6.3.

Continuity is true when the plan line reaches story 0 and no story on that line has an opening. The top of the wall is the top of that line. A wall that starts above story 0, or an opening on any story, is not continuous. Two walls listing the same wall_above, or a cycle, throw. Walking down to the base and back up uses separate seen sets, so a middle story is the same line as the base.

The decision is recorded on OrdinaryWallResult.boundary for solid piers on A1 or B4. Dead and wind combinations are not read. hu is this story's height. c is the largest converged §22.2 depth among earthquake combinations, from the compression face. A flange is used when one perpendicular wall meets an end. Thickness, ordinary checks, and reinforcement are unchanged. single_critical_section=true throws rather than inventing δu.

Tests: StructuralSynthesizer/test/analyze/test_special_boundary_stage.jl, 68/68, logs/test_StructuralSynthesizer_20261111_095050.log. Opening a stack flips only continuous. Stress cuts sit at may_discontinue, between, and required. Moment sign selects the end. Story height moves the thickness floor and not the zone length. A flange lowers the fiber stress. An A3 building records nothing; an A1 building records one decision per solid pier and does not change thickness.
