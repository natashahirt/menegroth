# Quote: plastic hinge regions shift elastic moment diagram

Moment redistribution is dependent on adequate ductility in plastic hinge regions. Plastic hinge regions develop
at sections of maximum positive or negative moment and cause a shift in the elastic moment diagram. The usual
result is a reduction in the values of maximum negative moments in the support regions and an increase in the
values of positive moments between supports from those computed by elastic analysis.
