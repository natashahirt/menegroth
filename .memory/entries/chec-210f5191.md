A grillage puts the floor pressure on each rib as an Asap.TributaryLoad (width times pressure, N/m). The scene now stores those breakpoints as tributary_load_element, tributary_load_position, and tributary_load. Dead and live loads on the same member are summed. The Loads toggle draws them with the other line-load arrows, on one shared scale, and draws nothing outside the breakpoint range.
