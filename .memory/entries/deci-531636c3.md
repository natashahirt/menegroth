Every wall shell node at the support elevation is fixed in all six degrees of freedom inside to_asap!, after the passes that free rotations and before the rigid-diaphragm links. Upper-story walls are not held. A skeleton with no support vertices is unchanged.

This is the rigid mat or strip under a grade wall. The mesh itself still creates free nodes; the restraint is applied in the builder. Nodes between support vertices no longer hang.

The ordinary-wall fixtures that needed a flexible building were retuned. They had been relying on the rocking base. The P-delta office softens its X piers by 0.4, a fixture factor, so θ stays in (0.10, θmax]. The Y-weak office puts the frame weak axis in Y, and θ then exceeds θmax again. test_ordinary_wall_stage.jl is 403/403. The wall-assembly test expects the created base nodes to be fixed.

Wall strip and core mat sizing is not built yet. deci-67cf61cb still stands: a joined core shares one mat, an isolated wall gets a strip, and punching at a wall stays ungrounded until the corpus supports it.
