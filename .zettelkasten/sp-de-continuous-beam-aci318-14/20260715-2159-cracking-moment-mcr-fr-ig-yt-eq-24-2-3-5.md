# Cracking moment Mcr = fr·Ig/yt (Eq. 24.2.3.5b)

Cracking moment. LaTeX: \( M_{cr} = \dfrac{f_r\,I_g}{y_t} \). Symbols: \(f_r\) = modulus of rupture (psi), \(I_g\) = gross inertia (in⁴), \(y_t\) = distance from centroid to tension fiber (in). Worked: Mcr = 474.34×18,432/12 (÷12,000 for kip-ft) = 60.72 kip-ft. Clause ACI 318-14 Eq. 24.2.3.5b, page 17.
