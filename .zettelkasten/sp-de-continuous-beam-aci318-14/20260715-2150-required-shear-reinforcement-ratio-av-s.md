# Required shear reinforcement ratio (Av/s)req = 0.0328 in2/in (§22.5.10.5.3)

Required stirrup area per unit length (Av/s)req = 0.0328 in²/in (per ACI 318-14 §22.5.10.5.3), governing over minimum 0.0133 in²/in. With Av = 0.40 in², demand-based spacing s = 0.40/0.0328 = 12.21 in. Clause 22.5.10.5.3, page 15.
