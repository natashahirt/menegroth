# Quote: continuous T-beam demonstration scope

The continuous beam along grid 3 is selected to demonstrate the analysis and design of continuous T-beams (structural
analysis, flexural design, shear design, deflection checks) and the results of hand calculations are then compared with
numerical analysis results obtained from the spBeam engineering software program.
