# Moment distribution vs moment redistribution distinction

The example distinguishes moment distribution (a structural-analysis method for statically indeterminate beams/frames) from moment redistribution (behavior of not-fully-elastic indeterminate structures with reserve plastic capacity, where first yielding causes moments to redistribute away from the elastic prediction).
