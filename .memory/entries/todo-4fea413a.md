# C3 — Wood roof framing

Sloped/light wood roofs — rafters, ridge, purlins, and pre-fabricated wood trusses — cover single-family, small hotel, and many restaurants. Nothing exists today.

**Build:** rafter/purlin bending+shear+deflection (NDS), sloped-load resolution, and light wood-truss member/joint design (metal-plate-connected truss analysis as an assembly of NDS members). **Depends on:** Phase E1/E2 (NDS 2018) and D1 (timber member engine). **Couples to:** A6 (snow), Phase F4 (wood typologies).
