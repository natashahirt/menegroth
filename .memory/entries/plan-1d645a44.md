Edition: ACI 318-19 for all detailing, matching the user's earlier choice of 318-19 throughout. Slab flexure stays 318-11 until ported; that mismatch is reported.

Cost: detailing runs once after sizing converges, before capture_design, never inside the MIP or NLP. Detail once per unique key (section, span, support conditions, demand envelope) and reuse across members. Store bars as compact bar sets (one shape plus pitch and count), with length and mass computed in closed form. Expand to 3D polylines only when serializing. The solve keeps its current per-candidate steel estimate; capture reports the detailed total next to it.

Phases:
0. Fix the red column spacing tests (1e-9 tolerance, NLP sweep sections where no layout fits). Build a shared detailing core: hooks and bend diameters (25.3), straight and hooked development (25.4), lap splices (25.5), clear spacing (25.2), bar sets, lengths and masses.
1. Two-way slabs from strip designs: Fig. 8.7.4.1.3 extensions (aci-318-19 p.118), longer-span rule, integrity bars through the column core, transfer bars within c2+3h, exterior corner reinforcement, drop panels, edge hooks, laps, and layer heights matching the design's outer and inner layers. The takeoff replaces the full-span strip estimate. Shell areas per element become bar zones later.
2. Beams: port shear to 318-19, stirrup zones (up to 3 per half-span, spacing rounded down to 1 in), cutoffs from M(x), continuing bars at supports, hooked anchorage at exterior columns, structural integrity, skin reinforcement, crack-control spacing, T-beam flange bars, OMF 18.3.2.
3. Columns: tie size, spacing, crossties, first tie, ties through joints, lap splices above each floor, offset bends or dowels at size changes, circular spirals, OMF 18.3.3.
4. Footings: bar runs, end hooks where development is short, dowels into the column.
5. Joints: clash and clear-spacing check where beam bars from two directions meet column bars. It is reported, not fed back into sizing.
6. Bar schedule with marks, API geometry, docs, and takeoff/EC from bar sets.

Judgment: 60 ft stock length (project option). Bottom laps over supports, top laps near midspan. Straight bars when the embedment fits, otherwise a 90 deg hook. Chairs are left out of the takeoff.
