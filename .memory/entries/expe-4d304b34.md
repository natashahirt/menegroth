Two additions on top of R.1/R.3, plus two important measurement bugs caught and fixed.

**1. Certified lower bound (Pillar A).** Added `catalog_lower_envelope` + `relaxation_lower_bound` + `optimality_gap`. BUG CAUGHT: my first lower bound summed `continuous_area` (the least-squares power-law fit) — which is NOT a lower envelope. On the real W catalog the continuation sweep reported a -33% ‘gap’ (bound ABOVE the discrete volume) — a FALSE optimality certificate, unacceptable in safety-critical code. Replaced with the mathematically correct bound: the CONVEX LOWER HULL of the catalog's (I,A) points (Andrew monotone chain), taking the min of the convex envelope over the feasible tail I≥I_target (robust to dips where a stiffer section is also lighter). Softened `continuous_area` docstring to ‘price/estimate, not a bound’. Validated: new tests assert envelope ≤ lightest-feasible on (a) a deliberately SCATTERED two-family synthetic catalog and (b) the real 283-section W catalog across 60 targets. 193/193 green.

**2. Continuation primitive (Pillar B).** `scripts/runners/relaxation_continuation_sweep.jl`: sweep a demand factor α, warm-start each step from the previous design (step 1 from R.1 projection). All 7 steps byte-identical to cold. The certified gap is positive (valid) but LOOSE (~116-140%) — the convex frontier is optimistic on a sparse catalog; honest limitation, not hidden.

**3. TIMING METHODOLOGY (critical lesson).** Julia JIT makes naive @elapsed on MIP solves treacherous. Measured speedup went ×1.86 (small-warmup: cold-path compile inflated cold) → ×1.07 (full-size cold-only warmup: mip_start PATH compiled during its own timed solve, masking the win) → ×15.5 warm / ×56 band (RIGOROUS: warm up EVERY solver path — cold, mip_start, feasible — at full size with perturbed demands so JIT is fully excluded and no memo key is pre-cached). The rigorous numbers on the 30-group/283-section benchmark: cold 5,338 ms → mip_start 344 ms (×15.5, byte-identical) → feasible band k=2 95 ms (×56, 0% gap, identical). So warm-start DOES give a large genuine speedup on non-trivial MIPs; my intermediate ‘no speedup’ report to the user was itself a compilation artifact and was corrected.

Must-warm-all-paths rule now baked into both runners. Still zero edits to the other session's size_*.jl; owner plumbing still deferred.
