# Averaging Ie over positive & negative regions (§24.2.3.6, §24.2.3.7)

ACI 318-14 §24.2.3.7 permits Ie from Eq. 24.2.3.5a at midspan for simple/continuous spans (at support for cantilevers). §24.2.3.6 permits, for continuous one-way slabs/beams, taking Ie as the average of values from Eq. 24.2.3.5a for the critical positive and negative moment regions. Clauses 24.2.3.6 & 24.2.3.7, page 16.
