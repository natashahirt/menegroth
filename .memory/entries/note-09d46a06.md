A horizontal segment whose clear span is longer than four times its depth is checked with the existing beam designers. Flexure is design_beam_flexure (ACI 318-11 §10.2 and §10.5). Shear is design_beam_shear (ACI 318-11 §11.2), including the axial force from the same combination. The width is the wall thickness and the depth is the band height. Effective depth deducts the 1½ in beam cover from ACI 318-19 Table 20.5.1.3.1 (aci-318-19, PDF p. 384) plus the #3 stirrup and ½ in bar the flexure function already assumes. The check passes when the bars that function selects provide the area it requires. A shorter band stays recorded unchecked: ACI 318-19 §9.9.1.1 (PDF p. 154) makes it a deep beam, and there is no strut-and-tie solver. The gravity-only in-plane sway magnifier is unchanged. The column function needs the nonsway and sway moments separately, and the wall story stiffness still requires rigid-diaphragm masters.
