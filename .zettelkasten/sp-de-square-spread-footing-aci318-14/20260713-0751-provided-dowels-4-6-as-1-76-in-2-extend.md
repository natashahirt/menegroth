# Provided dowels 4 #6 (As=1.76 in.^2), extend 23 in. into column

Four #6 corner dowels providing 1.76 in.^2 are used, extending the greater of a 23 in. compression splice or the 20 in. #9 compression-development length (per ACI 318-14 §25.5.5.4) into the column, and 16 in. into the footing. Test-oracle output.
