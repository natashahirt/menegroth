Agreed with the user 2026-11-18 after expe-a771f11b (30 in waffle stopped at the 35% solid cap with punching 2.3-3.3).

- A column-face punching failure makes the solid head deeper (a drop below the rib soffit), not wider. Spreading bays cannot change the section at d/2 from the column.
- The head's plan extent is set only by the ribs leaving it. Each rib is checked on its own section and its own shear at d from the face, not the narrowest section against the largest shear.
- Void closure applies to pans that cannot be formed, not to pans narrowed by ribs widened in the same pass.

First step: a diagnostic of which check governs each head, the rib section and shear behind the one-way ratio, and how many bays came from void closure versus growth.
