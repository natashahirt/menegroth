# Truss-connection resistance factors by limit state (§K3.3)

For rectangular HSS-to-HSS truss connections (§K3.3, Table K3.2), resistance factors depend on the limit state: chord wall plastification uses φ=0.90 (LRFD)/Ω=1.67 (ASD), while shear yielding (punching) and local yielding of branches use φ=0.95 (LRFD)/Ω=1.58 (ASD).
