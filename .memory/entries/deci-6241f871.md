Context: grid/x1y1 steel two-way stopped with every preferred W failing deflection_ll and deflection_total (closest W36X652 at 1.72 x L/360 and 3.39 x L/240) on a 6.0 m group whose strength demand needs about a W21. The frame path (`member_group_demands` -> `_service_load_deflections`) takes the largest ABSOLUTE vertical displacement per element, divides by the member's span with member ends always treated as supports, and scales by I_ref/Ix of the candidate. On grid/x1y1, 16 of 25 grid points are beam crossings with no column, so support movement of unsized (W10x22 placeholder) neighbours is charged to the member and cannot be scaled away. The story-shell path already uses `StructuralSizer.chord_deflection` (relative to supports). Concrete beams use the ACI checker's own deflection and are not affected.

User decisions (2026-11-17):
1. Reference: deflection of each span is measured relative to the line through its support points. A girder's sag is checked on the girder, not added to the members it carries. Cantilever: tip relative to the support, l = twice the cantilever length (IBC 2021 Table 1604.3 footnote i, corpus ibc-2021, PDF p. 418).
2. Crossings with no column: the member at the higher framing level (girder over beam over joist) supports the lower one. At the same level neither supports the other; both spans run to the next real support. ENGINEERING JUDGMENT, not a code statement.
3. L/240 for steel: AISC 360-16 Commentary L2 (corpus aisc-360-16, PDF p. 535): D is the dead load applied after nonstructural elements are attached. Implemented as live plus the cell superimposed dead load (`cell.sdl`), self-weight excluded. Treating all SDL as post-attachment is ENGINEERING JUDGMENT. IBC Table 1604.3 footnotes d and g would allow live only; Tan (MEng 2016) used immediate D+L, so comparisons to her shift.

Plan: (1) diagnostic runner splitting the failing group's deflection into own bending vs support movement, no code change; (2) one support/span definition shared by deflection and strength lengths; (3) chord-relative frame deflection for bare and composite steel incl. unshored split; (4) scaled search then unscaled re-check of the converged design, no placeholder sections in the check; (5) L/240 load per decision 3; (6) tests: beam on two columns unchanged, beam on flexible girder, cantilever, small crossing grid; (7) rerun grid/x1y1 steel two-way then deck.
