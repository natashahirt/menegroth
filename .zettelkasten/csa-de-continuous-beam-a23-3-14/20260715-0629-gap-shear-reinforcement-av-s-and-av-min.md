# GAP — shear reinforcement Av/s and Av,min (§11.3.5.1, §11.3.6.2, §11.2.8.2)

Required Av/s = (Vf − Vc)/(φs fyt dv cot θ) with θ = 35° (§11.3.6.2) per §11.3.5.1, and Av,min = 0.06√f'c bw/fyt (§11.2.8.2, here 0.30 mm²/mm); the printed equations are OCR-garbled.
