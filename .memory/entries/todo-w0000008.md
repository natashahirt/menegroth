# Out-of-plane / slender wall design (bearing & tilt-up)

Today the wall stack is purely **in-plane** (shear now, flexure via w0000007).
Real walls also carry gravity + out-of-plane (wind/eccentric axial) demand and
are frequently **slender**, where second-order (P–Δ) effects govern. The corpus
has three worked examples for this axis.

**Build** (new `StructuralSizer/src/walls/out_of_plane.jl`):
- Empirical/simplified axial method — ACI 318-19 §11.5.3.1 Eq. (11-5)
  `φPn,max = 0.55·φ·f'c·Ag·[1 − (k·lc/(32h))²]` (bearing walls).
- Alternative method for slender walls — ACI 318-19 §11.8: cracked stiffness
  `Icr` (§11.8.3.1(d), `Icr = (Ase·(lw... )` — see the effective-flexural-stiffness
  guide, Eq. 6), moment magnification, and the **service out-of-plane deflection
  limit Δs ≤ lc/150** (§11.8.1.1). Uses `WALL_ISTIFF_*` gross values already in
  `wall_section.jl`.
- P–Δ iteration for the midheight section; axial cap `Pu ≤ 0.06·f'c·Ag`
  (§11.8.1.1(d)).
- **Tilt-up** is the slender case with construction/lifting stages; share the
  §11.8 machinery, add panel self-weight + suction load cases later.

**Known-answer oracles** (corpus):
- `sp-de-bearing-wall-aci19` — simplified §11.5.3 bearing wall.
- `sp-de-multi-story-solid-aci19` — slender, multi-story, §11.8 (has Mu/Δ per
  story: e.g. midheight Mu, `Δz,service` targets).
- `sp-de-tilt-up-wall-aci19` — tilt-up panel, alternative method + Icr.

Depends on w0000007 (shares the P–M section engine). Feeds gravity/eccentric
demand from the (future) pipeline integration.
