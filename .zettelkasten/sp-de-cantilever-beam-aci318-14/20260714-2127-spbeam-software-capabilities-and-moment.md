# spBeam Software Capabilities and Moment Redistribution

spBeam performs analysis/design of beams and one-way slab systems per ACI 318-14 and CSA A23.3-14, integrating up to 20 spans and two cantilevers, and provides bar detailing, deflection results, and moment redistribution allowing up to 20% reduction of negative moments over supports.
