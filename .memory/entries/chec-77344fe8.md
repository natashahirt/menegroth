Pierced special piers no longer refuse horizontal segments. `coupling_beams.jl` designs them and `check_coupling_beam` files the records.

- ℓn/h ≥ 4 is §18.6, including §18.6.2.1(a) and (b). The beam is the wall thickness, so it does not project past the wall boundary.
- The beams §18.10.7.3 leaves either way use §18.6.3 through §18.6.5 and skip the width and span limits. A section that cannot carry the load is a failed record, not a switch to diagonals and not a throw.
- ℓn/h < 2 with Vu at or above 4λ√f′c·Acw is §18.10.7.4(a)–(c). φ = 0.85 (§21.2.4.4). Each group is at least four bars in two layers. α comes from the minimum cage: inset = 1.5 in cover + (bw/5)/2, arm = h − 2·inset, α = atan(arm/ℓn).
- Ve = (Mpr_start + Mpr_end)/ℓn + Vg. Mpr is strain compatibility at 1.25 fy. Vg is the largest factored gravity shear plus 0.2·SDS times the unfactored dead shear. Beam shear φ stays 0.75 because Vn is set from Mpr, above the shear at Mn.
- Vc = 0 in the hoop zones only when the probable-moment shear is at least half of Ve and Pu is less than Ag f′c/20.
