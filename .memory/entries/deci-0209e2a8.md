Seismic Design Categories D, E, and F now calculate the moments and shears that the design earthquake displacement produces in the reinforced-concrete gravity frame. The drift-case displacement is scaled by Cd/Ie (ASCE 7-22 Eq. 12.8-16, δdi = 0). When those forces do not exceed the design strength at the gravity axial, the member is detailed to ACI 318-19 §18.14.3.2. Otherwise, and when the displacement or a frame element is missing, it stays on §18.14.3.3.

A special wall with a known §18.10.6.4 length now places closed hoops at both ends. A hoop leg is at most twice the thickness, so a longer element uses overlapping hoops. A crosstie crosses the thickness at every longitudinal bar that is not a hoop corner. The vertical spacing follows §18.10.6.4(e), tighter inside the greater of ℓw and Mu/(4Vu).

§18.14.3.2(c), half of Table 18.7.5.4 when the gravity axial exceeds 0.35P0, is reported and not placed. The §26.11 gust-factor stack is not coded. The ordinary-wall wind path stays at 0.15B until eR, Iz, gQ, Q, gR, and R are supplied.

Tests: StructuralSizer detailing 293/293. StructuralSynthesizer ordinary-wall stage 678/678, including the boundary-hoop notes. The ordinary-wall fixtures copy the story-1 beam plan and faces down to the column bases, because story 0 is no longer given a beam grid.
