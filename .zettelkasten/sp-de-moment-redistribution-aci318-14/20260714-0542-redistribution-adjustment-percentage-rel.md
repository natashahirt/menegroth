# Redistribution adjustment percentage relation

The allowable redistribution adjustment percentage equals 1000 times the section net tensile strain, limited to a maximum of 20%. LaTeX: \text{Adjustment}(\%) = \min(1000\,\varepsilon_t,\ 20). Symbols: ε_t = net tensile strain (in./in.), Adjustment = percent reduction of the factored moment.
