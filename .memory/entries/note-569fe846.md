The 627 kip·ft interior negative was the equivalent frame treating a wall that covers only part of the strip as a fixed joint, and keeping a span that lies on a parallel wall. After those two changes the frame envelope on the open floor (z = 3.6 m, h = 10.5 in, M0 = 594.6 kip·ft) is M−ext = M−int = 577.9 kip·ft and M+ = 330.3 kip·ft. That negative is about M0, which is what a stiff wall support produces at the near end of a span.

The shell column envelope is unchanged: M−ext = 187.7, M−int = 0 (every column is an edge column), M+ = 321.4. Hogging integrated along the wall face, not scaled up to the whole floor, is 704.5 kip·ft at (8, 12) m and 617.6 kip·ft at (16, 12) m, each wall 8 m long. Those are the numbers to set next to the frame's wall-joint moment. An earlier print of about 1686 kip·ft had spread the wall's average intensity across every element in the cut.

DDM still refuses the floor under §13.6.1.1 and §13.6.1.2.
