User asked to apply Phase 6d on 2026-10-25.

Scope of this run: seismic ELF only. Wind story forces are not encoded (no wind.jl; Chapter 27 was read and not promoted into Julia).

Story forces go on the existing rigid-diaphragm masters, as separate load cases from gravity, batched with Asap.solve(model, cases). Accidental torsion is additional cases: the story force plus a moment F*e at the master, both signs. Asap.solve_pdelta! calls solve! without the rigid links, so it is not the P-delta solve for this slice. P-delta is the §12.8.7 stability coefficient and displacement multiplier on the linear rigid-diaphragm results. If θ exceeds θmax, the structure shall be redesigned. A geometric P-delta solve waits until it can keep the rigid links. apply_wall_cache_class is not called. The seismic-weight to sizing to weight loop stays open for Phase 7.

Ie, R, SDS, S1, SD1, T, TL, and Cd are caller inputs. SiteConditions.risk_category is not used to infer them.
