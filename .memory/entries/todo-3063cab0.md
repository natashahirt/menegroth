# C5 — Pre-engineered metal buildings (PEMB)

PEMBs (tapered-web rigid frames + girts/purlins + metal panel) are a dominant warehouse/industrial system. Requires **web-tapered member** design (AISC 360 App. behavior / AISC Design Guide 25) which our prismatic-section machinery does not cover.

**Build:** tapered I-section geometry + stability/flexure for variable-depth members, moment-frame analysis, cold-formed girts/purlins (couples to D2), and the standing-seam/panel roof. Lower near-term priority — a specialized track — but required to fully cover the DOE Warehouse alt. **Depends on:** AISC DG25 + D2 (CFS) canon in Phase E.
