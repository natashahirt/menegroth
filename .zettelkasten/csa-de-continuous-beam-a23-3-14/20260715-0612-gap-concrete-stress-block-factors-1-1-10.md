# GAP — concrete stress-block factors α1, β1 (§10.1.7)

α1 and β1 are computed from f'c per CSA A23.3-14 §10.1.7 (α1 = 0.85 − 0.0015f'c ≥ 0.67; β1 = 0.97 − 0.0025f'c ≥ 0.67), but the printed equation lines are OCR-garbled (space-split digits).
