# D8 — Structural fire design

Today fire is handled as (a) ACI 216.1 minimum thickness/cover for concrete (`codes/aci/fire.jl`) and (b) fireproofing embodied-carbon accounting (SFRM/intumescent on steel). There is **no elevated-temperature structural design** — steel strength at temperature, membrane action, or performance-based fire.

**Build:** AISC 360-16 **Appendix 4** (structural design for fire conditions — retention factors, simple/advanced methods), concrete thermal strength reduction, and required fire-resistance ratings driven by IBC occupancy (E9). Prescriptive ratings first; performance-based later. **Canon:** ACI 216.1 (in corpus), AISC 360 App. 4 (verify in corpus / ingest), IBC Ch. 6–7 (E9). **Couples to:** E9 (IBC ratings), existing fireproofing-EC path.
