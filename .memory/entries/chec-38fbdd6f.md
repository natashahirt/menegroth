Vertical segments of a pierced special wall are designed for Ve and the §21.2.4.1 φ. αc uses the greater of the entire-wall and segment hw/ℓw ratios (§18.10.4.2). Ωv and ωv use the full line height, which is at least the height above the segment. The §18.10.4.3 floor applies when either ratio is 2 or less. A flanged segment with hwcs/ℓw > 1.5 still throws, because Mpr would omit the flange bars. Horizontal segments still refuse: they are coupling beams under §18.10.7 and that reinforcement is not built. Fresh special-shear and boundary-stage tests: 480/480.
