# Longitudinal bar spacing limits (Table 24.3.2, 24.3.2.1)

Maximum spacing of bonded longitudinal reinforcement per Table 24.3.2 is the lesser of 15(40000/fs) − 2.5cc and 12(40000/fs), with fs permitted to be taken as 2/3 fy = 40,000 psi per 24.3.2.1.
