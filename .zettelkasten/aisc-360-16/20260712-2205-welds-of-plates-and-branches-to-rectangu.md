# Welds of plates and branches to rectangular HSS (§K5)

AISC 360-16 §K5 sizes welds of plates and branches to rectangular HSS accounting for the nonuniformity of load transfer along the weld line (differences in relative HSS-wall stiffness), requiring interaction of axial and bending weld demands.
