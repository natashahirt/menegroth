Accepted 2026-10-28. Ten floors in three families. Post-tensioned, hollow-core, slab on grade, timber, joist roofs, and non-composite deck stay stubs.

Already designed: flat plate, flat slab with drops, vault.

Finish next (steel): composite steel deck on steel beams (parallel, perpendicular, or skewed ribs; beams need not be orthogonal). Remaining: station-varying deflection, and a floor entry point that still errors. Solid concrete slab on steel beams is the same beam check with no ribs; the slab bars are not designed.

Then concrete slab-and-rib, one pour. Solid one-way and two-way slabs on beams are the parents (thickness only today). Parallel ribs are one-way joists (T-beams). Waffle is a regular two-way rib grid. Arbitrary rib network is ribs at any orientation, each panel one-way or two-way from its shape. Courtyards stay parked: a void is still poured as a panel unless marked.

Shared skeleton already built: faces from the beam graph, tributary width along each beam, frame analysis, concrete T-beams when the flange is turned on, steel composite beams with a solid slab or a deck. Missing for every concrete system in this family: design of the slab panel itself.
