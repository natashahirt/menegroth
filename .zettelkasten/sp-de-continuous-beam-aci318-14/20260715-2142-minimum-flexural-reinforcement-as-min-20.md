# Minimum flexural reinforcement As,min = (200/fy)·bw·d, §9.6.1.2(b)

Minimum flexural steel, term (b). LaTeX: \( A_{s,min} = \dfrac{200}{f_y}\,b_w\,d \). Symbols: \(f_y\) = 60,000 psi, \(b_w\) = 16 in., \(d\) = 22 in. Worked: 200/60,000×16×22 = 1.17 in². This term governs over term (a): As,min = 1.17 in². Clause ACI 318-14 §9.6.1.2(b), page 11.
