User chose (2026-11-17) the usual waffle practice: extend the solid head until the ribs leaving it carry the shear on concrete alone.

Why: the head check compares rib shear at d from the head face with φVc (ACI 318-11 §11.11.1.1, §11.1.3.1; §8.13.8 10 percent for joists). The beam sizer checks the same rib against φ(Vc + Vs) with Vs up to 8√f'c bw d (t_checker.jl). A rib the sizer passes can read up to about 4.6–5 against φVc. The 30 in qualification rerun stopped at 4.89 (Slab 13), every slab governed by rib_one_way with 0 growth steps. Feeding the failing shear back to the sizer (deci-98b1d8e2) could not lower it further, and the pending hold kept the beam/column loop from settling ("did not settle within max_iterations").

Rule: a head grows when column punching fails or a rib leaving it fails one-way shear. The step follows the column punching face when column punching fails; otherwise the outward direction of the governing one-way ribs, or one ring when they name none (deci-1b3809c2). A step is kept when the larger of the column punching ratio and the rib one-way ratio falls. Stem compression (rib_hogging) still does not grow the head.

Supersedes deci-98b1d8e2: the stored rib shear, the beam demand it raised, and the loop hold are removed. No new formula.
