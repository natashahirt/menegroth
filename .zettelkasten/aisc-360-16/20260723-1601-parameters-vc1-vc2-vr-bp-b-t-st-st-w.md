# Parameters Vc1, Vc2, Vr, bp, (b/t)st, ρst, ρw

Vc1 = available strength per G2.1/G2.2; Vc2 = available strength with Vn=0.6FyAwCv2; Vr = required shear; bp = smaller of a and h; ρst = larger of Fyw/Fyst and 1.0; ρw = max (Vr−Vc2)/(Vc1−Vc2) ≥ 0.
