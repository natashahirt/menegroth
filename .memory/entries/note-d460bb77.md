ACI 318-11 §12.12.1 (corpus: aci-318-11, page 230): top bars at a wall are anchored by embedment or a standard hook. Straight ℓd is §12.2.2 "other cases" (page 214), ψt = 1.3 because the wall is cast with the slab (§12.2.4(a), page 215), ψe = 1.0, and ℓd is not less than 12 in. The hook is §12.5.2 (page 217) with no §12.5.3 reduction. Available length is the wall thickness minus clear cover. A bar that fits neither is a recorded failure and does not increase the slab thickness.

For Grade 60, f'c = 4000 psi, λ = 1, and a 0.30 m wall with 0.75 in cover: a #4 straight bar needs 37.0 in and does not fit; its hook needs 9.5 in and does fit. A #8 hook does not fit.
