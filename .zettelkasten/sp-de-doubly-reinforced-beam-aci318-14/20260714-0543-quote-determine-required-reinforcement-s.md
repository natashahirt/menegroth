# Quote: Determine required reinforcement steel area

Determine the required reinforcement steel area for a concrete beam carrying service dead and live loads. First, check
if singly reinforced beam section is suitable. If not, try doubly reinforced concrete beam section by adding compression
reinforcement. It is desired that the section for this cantilever beam be tension controlled.
