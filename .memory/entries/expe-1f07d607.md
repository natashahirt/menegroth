scripts/runners/core_office_wall_jump.jl on core_office_skeleton, gravity combinations only, jump_wall_thickness true. No wind or earthquake.

Start 4 in: 4 in → 5 in → 6 in, then the two-remesh cap. ρl fell as t grew. Roof vertices for interior wall tops were missing; _seed_roof_wall_vertices! in core_office_geometry.jl adds them so to_asap! can mesh the top story. That seed is not yet covered by a fresh test_core_shaft run.

Start 6 in (above the Table 11.3.1.1 estimate): 6 in → 7 in → 8 in, then the cap. At 8 in, 12 records still fail, all on two ground-story lobby/shaft segments — (8, 10.5)–(10.4, 10.5) and (12.8, 10.5)–(16, 10.5). Every failure is ACI 318-19 §11.5.3 net axial tension, method inapplicable (demand about −6 kN at the bottom and −59 kN at the top, ratio Inf). Other piers passed. max ρl at 8 in was 0.006 and max ρt 0.006.

The walk is one inch per remesh because a negative implied tributary axial makes wall_thickness_scale_applies false, and propose_wall_thickness then returns the next inch without a proxy pass. That is the opposite of the settled rule that a negative tributary does not jump. Thickening did not clear the tension, and the cap left the failures recorded.
