# Simplified coefficient method inapplicable — span ratio > 1.2 (§6.5.1)

The ACI 318-14 §6.5.1 approximate-coefficient method requires prismatic members, uniform loads, L ≤ 3D, at least two spans, and adjacent spans differing by ≤ 20%; here the adjacent-span ratio exceeds 1.2, so coefficients cannot be used and traditional (moment-distribution) analysis is required.
