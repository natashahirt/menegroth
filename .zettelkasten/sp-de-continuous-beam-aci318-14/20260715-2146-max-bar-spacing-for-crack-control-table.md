# Max bar spacing for crack control (Table 24.3.2)

Maximum center-to-center spacing of bonded flexural tension reinforcement. LaTeX: \( s = 15\left(\dfrac{40000}{f_s}\right) - 2.5 c_c \le 12\left(\dfrac{40000}{f_s}\right) \). Symbols: \(f_s\) = stress in reinforcement at service (psi), \(c_c\) = clear cover to bar surface (in). Worked with fs = 40,000 psi, cc = 1.5 in.: s = 15×1 − 2.5×1.5 = 11.25 in. (governs) ≤ 12 in. Clause ACI 318-14 Table 24.3.2, page 12.
