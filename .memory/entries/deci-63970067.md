User-confirmed 2026-11-07. Not yet implemented.

Classification of grade (story-0) walls:
- A connected group whose plan closes a cell -> one mat (bounding plan). Column supports inside the plan join the mat and keep column punching.
- A spur running out of a cell -> its own wall footing starting at the cell face (not on the mat).
- Any other connected group (straight run, L, T) -> ONE wall footing with legs. Corner condition = one rigid irregular combined footing (ACI 336.2R §4.2, p. 7; linear pressure §3.2/§4.1, pp. 6-7). Leg moments by cutting planes (ACI 318-11 §15.4.1, p. 272), so the corner node never needs splitting. Full contact only; partial contact on an irregular shape is refused (Eq. 3-2..3-4 are rectangle-only).
- A column at a wall end shares the wall node; its reaction becomes a sample of the wall line load. No punching there.

Design API: FoundationDemand stays a column point. New WallLineDemand (p1, p2, tw, samples s/Pu/Ps/M_axis, uplift, dowel As_min per length). design_footing(::StripFooting, legs::Vector{WallLineDemand}, soil) -> StripFootingResult with n_columns=0. design_footing(::MatFoundation, demands, positions, soil; walls=WallLineDemand[]); empty walls must reproduce today's mats. Projection: moment at wall face §15.4.2(a), one-way shear at d from face §15.5.2 (p. 273), bars uniform §15.4.3; development §15.6.3, 6 in. min §15.7, wall dowels §15.8.2.2. No punching at walls.

Mat: both RigidMat and WinklerFEA take walls (user picks via MatParams.analysis_method). Rigid strips per 336.2R §6.1.2 Step 3 (p. 11), valid only if spacing < 1.75/lambda (or very thick) and loads/spans vary <= 20%. Winkler: wall samples as nodal forces on mesh lines along each wall (Fig. 6.2(a) shows a mat with a wall). Eq. 6-3 pseudo-ultimate pressure.

Inapplicable rigid method -> switch automatically to Winkler and note it in the result, mirroring slabs' on_inapplicable=:fallback (FEA) with :error as the alternative (StructuralSizer/src/slabs/options.jl).

Corpus gotcha: the corpus TEXT garbles 336.2R Eq. 5-1 and 5-3. Read from rendered page images (p. 8-9): Kr = E'I_B / (E_s B^3) (5-1); E'I_B = E'I_F + sum E'I_b + E' t_w h_w^3 / 12, per unit width of building (5-2); rigid if Kr >= 0.5; lambda = (K_s / (4 E_c I))^(1/4) (5-3). Definitions of B in 5-1 and K_s in 5-3 are not given on those pages (open). Existing rigid mat computes Kr = Ec*Ig/(ks*B*L^3) and only warns; that is not Eq. 5-1.

Ordinary-wall grade bases are fixed (deci-531636c3); wall-base reactions live on fixed mesh nodes that initialize_supports! does not read yet.
