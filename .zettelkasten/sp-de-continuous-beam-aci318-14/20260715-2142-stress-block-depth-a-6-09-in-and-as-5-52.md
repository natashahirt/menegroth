# Stress-block depth a = 6.09 in. and As = 5.52 in2

With As = 5.52 in²: a = As·fy/(0.85·f′c·b) = 5.52×60,000/(0.85×4,000×16) = 6.09 in.; c = a/β1 = 6.09/0.85 = 7.17 in. Governing required steel As,req = 5.52 in² at interior negative region.
