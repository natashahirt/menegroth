# GAP — concrete shear resistance Vc with β = 0.18 (§11.3.6.3)

Concrete shear resistance Vc = φc λ β √f'c bw dv with simplified β = 0.18 per CSA A23.3-14 §11.3.6.3 (here Vc = 116.9 kN), but the printed equation is OCR-garbled (√ radical dropped, space-split digits).
