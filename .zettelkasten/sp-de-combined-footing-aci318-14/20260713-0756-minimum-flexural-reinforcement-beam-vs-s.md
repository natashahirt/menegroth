# Minimum flexural reinforcement — beam vs slab provisions (ACI 318-14 §9.6.1.2, §7.6.1.1)

Minimum flexural reinforcement is checked using either beam provisions (ACI 318-14 §9.6.1.2) or one-way slab provisions (ACI 318-14 §7.6.1.1); both are illustrated.
