Locked 2026-10-28 for the concrete slab panel, before that panel is designed.

Each panel is one-way or two-way on its own. With no axis, the choice is automatic. The short and long spans are the ones `SpanInfo` already measures. ACI 318-19 R8.3.1.2 (corpus: aci-318-19, PDF p. 103) says a panel whose long-to-short span ratio is greater than 2 uses the one-way rules of §7.3.1. At or below 2, the panel is two-way. One-way spans along the short direction, which is `SpanInfo.primary` when `axis` is omitted.

A caller-supplied `axis`, the same `(x, y)` unit direction `SpanInfo` already accepts, overrides that choice: the panel is one-way and spans along that axis. Two-way is the automatic result, not a second flag.
