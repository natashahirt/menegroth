Confirmed 2026-10-31. The concrete panel on beams gets all three analysis methods, mirroring flat plate (direct design, equivalent frame, shell). One design layer serves all three. Only the moments and shears change.

Classification is already in place. ACI 318-19 R8.3.1.2 (corpus: aci-318-19, PDF p. 103): long-to-short greater than 2 is one-way along the short span; at or below 2 the panel is two-way. A supplied direction forces one-way along that direction. The building design pass currently sets minimum thickness only (ACI 318-11 Table 9.5(a) for one-way).

Default. Coefficients where ACI 318-11 §8.3.3 allows them (two or more spans, adjacent spans within 20 percent, uniform load, unfactored live load no more than three times the dead load, prismatic members). Outside those limits the design uses the continuous strip, the same way a flat plate leaves the direct design method when the slab does not qualify. The shell is always available as the third method, and it is how a two-way panel is analyzed.

Build order:
1. Shared checks, written once. Positive and negative moment, one-way shear, bar size and spacing, shrinkage and temperature steel, and deflection. The minimum thickness already exists and may increase when strength or deflection requires it.
2. Coefficients. Hand-check oracles on a regular bay. Inapplicable layouts do not silently design; they switch to the strip.
3. Continuous strip. A unit-width beam on the supports the frame already has, including the live-load patterns. Checked against the coefficients on a bay where both are allowed.
4. Shell. The flat-plate shell model, integrated along the span, with shrinkage steel in the other direction. Checked against the strip. This is also the two-way panel analysis. A strip does not capture plate action in both directions.

Clause targets for the design layer stay those already scoped for this work (ACI 318-11): §8.3.3 coefficients, §8.3.1 elastic analysis, §8.11.2 live-load arrangements, Chapter 10 flexure, §7.12 shrinkage and temperature steel, §7.6.5 and §10.6 spacing, §11.2 one-way shear, Table 9.5(a) or §9.5.2.2 computed deflection. Re-read each clause from the corpus before writing it.

Parallel ribs, waffle, and an arbitrary rib network stay after this panel. They reuse these checks. They do not get their own moment methods.
