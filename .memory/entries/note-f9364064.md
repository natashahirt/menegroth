These six provisions are still owed. The gate in `require_seismic_wall_demand_provisions` only refuses a demand that has reached one of them. It does not retire the provision.

Account for each one when the building reaches it:

- Cracked stiffness, before any seismic wall force is used for design. ASCE 7-22 §12.7.3 (asce-7-22, PDF page 184) and ACI 318-19 Table 6.6.3.1.1(a) (aci-318-19, PDF page 78): the caller states uncracked 0.70Ig or cracked 0.35Ig, axial area stays 1.0Ag, and sustained lateral load divides I by (1 + βds). No default.
- ρ in Eh, when Seismic Design Category is D, E, or F. Eq. 12.4-3 (PDF page 183) and §12.3.4.2 (PDF page 182). Category B or C may keep ρ = 1.0 under §12.3.4.1.
- Ax on accidental torsion, when Category C–F has a Type 1 horizontal irregularity. §12.8.4.3 Eq. 12.8-15 (PDF page 187).
- Orthogonal directions, when §12.5.3 or §12.5.4 (PDF pages 183–184) does not permit one direction. Category B may stay independent.
- P-delta on member forces, when θ > 0.10. §12.8.7 (PDF page 188). At or below 0.10 the linear forces stand.
- Effective flange width, when a joined wall is designed. §18.10.5.2 (PDF page 325) is the special-wall width and is not applied to an ordinary wall. An ordinary joined assembly stays refused until a width for that wall is taken from the code.

The first ordinary wall, one seismic direction, Category B or C, can proceed inside those permissions. Gravity on the walls is still the next step.
